# Sapper

The Sapper is the **insurgent/militia equivalent** of the Combat Engineer, specializing in IEDs (Improvised Explosive Devices) and guerrilla demolitions. Available only to unconventional factions.

## Role Limitations

- **Specialist** role type
- Available to: Insurgents, Militia
- Limited per team

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- AKM or similar rifle
- IED explosive (1-2)
- Anti-personnel mine (1-2)
- Phone detonator
- Shovel (for digging)
- Field dressing

### IED Types
| IED | Use |
|-----|-----|
| **Standard IED** | Buried explosive, massive damage |
| **IED Drone** | Flying bomb (some factions) |

## IED Mechanics

### Placing IEDs
1. Select IED from inventory
2. Place on ground (or bury with shovel)
3. IED becomes nearly invisible when buried
4. Mark location for yourself
5. Detonate with phone when enemies near

### Detonation
- Equip phone detonator
- Must have line of sight (some factions)
- Or remote detonation (other factions)
- Massive explosion radius

### Damage Output
- **Kills infantry** in large radius
- **Destroys light vehicles** instantly
- **Heavily damages** APCs/IFVs
- **Can damage** MBTs

## Responsibilities

1. **Deny enemy routes** - IEDs on roads
2. **Defend objectives** - Mine approaches
3. **Ambush vehicles** - Road IEDs
4. **Harassment** - Keep enemies paranoid
5. **Asymmetric warfare** - Fight smart, not fair

## Tips for Success

### IED Placement
- **Roads** - Vehicles must use them
- **Chokepoints** - Predictable movement
- **Objective approaches** - Enemy will come
- **Buried is better** - Harder to spot
- **Mark your IEDs** - Don't forget location

### Patience Pays
- Don't detonate on one enemy
- Wait for vehicles or groups
- Let them commit to the kill zone
- Maximum damage, maximum chaos

### Phone Detonator
- Stay in detonation range
- Have eyes on IED if needed
- Pre-position for detonation
- Backup plan if you die

## Combat Tactics

### Vehicle Ambush
1. Scout common vehicle routes
2. Place IED on road (bury if possible)
3. Position with line of sight
4. Wait for high-value target
5. Detonate when vehicle is on IED
6. Escape immediately

### FOB Defense
- Mine approaches to your FOB
- Place IEDs on likely attack routes
- Create kill zones
- Coordinate with other defenders

### Guerrilla Harassment
- Plant IEDs near enemy FOBs
- Target logistics routes
- Make enemies paranoid
- Disrupt their operations

## Common Mistakes

- Detonating on single infantry
- Forgetting IED locations (teamkills)
- Placing obvious, unburied IEDs
- Not coordinating with team
- Getting caught placing IEDs

## Sapper vs Engineer

| Sapper (Insurgent) | Combat Engineer (Conventional) |
|--------------------|-------------------------------|
| IEDs (larger blast) | C4 (precise placement) |
| Guerrilla focus | Assault focus |
| Remote detonation | Direct detonation |
| Insurgent factions | Conventional factions |

## IED Counters

Enemies can counter IEDs:
- Visual spotting (look for disturbed ground)
- Shooting IEDs detonates them
- Engineers can disarm
- Vehicles can trigger them (sacrifice)

Hide your IEDs well and detonate before they're spotted.

## The Insurgent Mindset

As a Sapper, you're fighting a different war:
- You don't need to win firefights
- You need to **cost them tickets**
- Every vehicle destroyed hurts them
- Every paranoid enemy is slower
- Make them fear every road
