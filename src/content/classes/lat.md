# Light Anti-Tank (LAT)

The Light Anti-Tank soldier carries **disposable anti-tank weapons** like the M72 LAW or RPG-7. This role is essential for defending against enemy vehicles and can turn the tide of armored engagements.

## Role Limitations

- **Specialist** role type
- Usually **2-3 LAT per squad** available
- More common than HAT

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- Standard assault rifle
- Anti-tank rocket (M72 LAW, RPG-7, AT4, etc.)
- 1-2 rockets total
- 1x Fragmentation grenade
- 1x Smoke grenade
- Field dressing

### Launcher Types
| Weapon | Faction | Rockets |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **M72 LAW** | USA/CAF/AUS | 1 disposable |
| **AT4** | USA/UK | 1 disposable |
| **RPG-7** | Russia/Militia/INS | 2 rockets |
| **RPG-26** | Russia | 1 disposable |
| **Carl Gustav** | CAF/UK | 2 rockets |

## Understanding Penetration

LAT weapons have **limited penetration**:
- Effective against: Light vehicles, APCs, IFV sides/rear
- Limited against: MBT front/side armor
- Aim for: Rear, sides, tracks, engine

### Target Priority
| Target | Effectiveness |
|--------|--------------|
| Logistics trucks | Very effective |
| Technicals | Very effective |
| APCs | Effective (sides/rear) |
| IFVs | Limited (sides/rear only) |
| MBTs | Tracks/engine only |

## Responsibilities

1. **Protect squad from vehicles** - Your primary job
2. **Disable, don't destroy** - Track shots count
3. **Conserve ammo** - You have limited rockets
4. **Position for shots** - Flanks are better
5. **Communicate vehicle locations** - Even if you can't engage

## Tips for Success

### Shot Placement

**Best targets on vehicles:**
- **Rear armor** - Weakest point
- **Side armor** - Second best
- **Tracks/wheels** - Mobility kill
- **Engine** - Mobility + fire
- **Turret rear** - Can disable weapons

**Avoid:**
- Front armor on tanks
- Reactive armor panels
- Shooting through bushes/fences (detonation)

### Engagement Tactics
1. **Wait for the right shot** - Don't waste rockets
2. **Flank when possible** - Side/rear shots
3. **Let vehicles pass** - Shoot the rear
4. **Coordinate with other AT** - Focus fire
5. **Use terrain** - Shoot from cover

### Rocket Conservation
- You likely have only **1-2 rockets**
- Make every shot count
- Stay near **Riflemen** for resupply
- Fall back to ammo crates when empty

## Combat Tactics

### Ambush Position
1. Find a position vehicles will pass
2. Stay concealed until the shot
3. Aim for rear/side as vehicle passes
4. Relocate immediately after firing
5. Don't re-engage from same position

### Defensive AT
- Pre-position on likely vehicle routes
- Coordinate with HAT for combined fire
- Call out vehicles before engaging
- Prioritize threats to infantry (IFVs > Tanks)

### Urban AT Combat
- Rooftops give good angles
- Windows can obstruct rockets (backblast danger!)
- Alleys create kill zones
- Watch for backblast in enclosed spaces

## Backblast Warning

**Backblast kills teammates!**
- Clear area behind you before firing
- At least 10-20m clear
- Call "Backblast clear!" in local voice
- Never fire from inside rooms (unless window)

## Common Mistakes

- Shooting front armor on tanks
- Wasting rockets on infantry
- Forgetting backblast checks
- Not communicating vehicle positions
- Going alone to "hunt" vehicles

## Resupply Priority

Always resupply rockets when possible:
- Rifleman ammo bag = ~1 rocket
- FOB ammo crate = full resupply
- Vehicle ammo = varies

Stay with your squad - a LAT without rockets is just a worse Rifleman.
