# Heavy Anti-Tank (HAT)

The Heavy Anti-Tank soldier carries **powerful anti-tank missiles** capable of destroying Main Battle Tanks. This is one of the most valuable and limited roles in Squad.

## Role Limitations

- **Specialist** role type
- **2 HAT per team maximum**
- Highly contested role - use it wisely

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- Compact/PDW rifle (limited range)
- Heavy AT launcher (Javelin, TOW, Kornet, etc.)
- 1-2 missiles
- 1x Smoke grenade
- Field dressing

### Launcher Types
| Weapon | Faction | Guidance | Missiles |
|--------|---------|----------|----------|
| **FGM-148 Javelin** | USA | Fire-and-forget | 1 |
| **NLAW** | UK | Fire-and-forget | 1 |
| **9M133 Kornet** | Russia | Wire-guided | 2 |
| **TOW** | USA (emplacement) | Wire-guided | - |
| **RPG-29** | Russia/INS | Tandem unguided | 2 |
| **Carl Gustav M4** | CAF | Tandem unguided | 2 |

## Missile Types Explained

### Fire-and-Forget (Javelin, NLAW)
- Lock onto target before firing
- Missile guides itself
- You can relocate immediately
- Top-attack mode defeats reactive armor

### Wire-Guided (Kornet)
- Must keep aiming until impact
- More vulnerable while guiding
- Very accurate at all ranges
- Can be interrupted if you die

### Tandem Warhead (RPG-29)
- Defeats reactive armor
- Unguided - requires aim skill
- Multiple rockets carried
- Good penetration

## Responsibilities

1. **Destroy enemy armor** - Your primary purpose
2. **Prioritize high-value targets** - MBTs, IFVs
3. **Stay alive** - You're extremely valuable
4. **Communicate armor locations** - Even if can't engage
5. **Conserve missiles** - Every shot matters

## Target Priority

| Priority | Target | Reason |
|----------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Enemy MBT | Biggest threat, only you can kill it |
| 2 | Enemy IFV | Threatening to infantry |
| 3 | Enemy APC | Still dangerous |
| 4 | Enemy Helicopter | If opportunity presents |

**DO NOT WASTE** missiles on:
- Logistics trucks (LAT can handle)
- Technicals (LAT can handle)
- Infantry (never)

## Tips for Success

### Using the Javelin
1. Find target and aim down sight
2. Wait for lock-on tone
3. Select attack mode (top-attack preferred)
4. Fire and immediately relocate
5. Missile self-guides

### Using Wire-Guided (Kornet)
1. Set up in stable position
2. Aim at target
3. Fire and **keep aiming**
4. Guide missile to impact
5. Don't move until hit

### General Tactics
- **Stay behind your squad** - You're too valuable
- **Never be alone** - You need protection
- **Communicate everything** - Armor spotted, missile fired
- **Flank for better shots** - Rear armor is best
- **Patience** - Wait for the right shot

## Combat Positioning

### Ideal HAT Position
- 200-400m from expected armor
- Concealed, not just covered
- Clear line of sight
- Escape route planned
- Squad nearby for protection

### What to Avoid
- Being in the front line
- Isolated positions
- Obvious AT positions (every HAT uses them)
- Enclosed spaces (backblast)

## Resupply is Critical

HAT missiles are expensive to resupply:
- Javelin missile = ~60 ammo points
- Rifleman bag = ~100 points (barely enough)
- You NEED FOB ammo crates
- Plan your logistics

## Common Mistakes

- Wasting missiles on light vehicles
- Being too aggressive/exposed
- Not communicating with team
- Lone wolfing (you need protection)
- Forgetting your rifle is weak

## Working with Vehicles

Coordinate with friendly armor:
- They spot, you shoot
- They engage, you flank for shot
- Call out armor for them
- Don't shoot friendlies (check your targets!)

## The HAT Paradox

You are simultaneously:
- The most **important** person against armor
- The most **vulnerable** person in your squad
- **Protected** by teammates
- **Protecting** teammates from tanks

Stay alive, make your shots count, communicate constantly.
