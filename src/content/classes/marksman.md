# Marksman

The Marksman carries a **Designated Marksman Rifle (DMR)** with a magnified optic, providing accurate fire at medium to long range. This role bridges the gap between standard riflemen and true snipers.

## Role Limitations

- **Fire Support** role type
- Max **3 Fire Support roles** per squad
- Shares pool with: Automatic Rifleman, Machine Gunner, Grenadier

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- Designated Marksman Rifle (M110, SVD, L129A1, etc.)
- 4x-8x magnified optic
- 10-20 round magazines
- 1x Fragmentation grenade
- 1x Smoke grenade
- Field dressing

### Weapon Characteristics
| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Caliber** | 7.62x51mm NATO / 7.62x54R |
| **Fire mode** | Semi-automatic |
| **Magazine** | 10-20 rounds |
| **Effective range** | 400-800m |
| **Optic** | 4x-8x variable |

## Marksman vs Sniper

| Marksman | Sniper |
|----------|--------|
| Semi-auto DMR | Bolt-action rifle |
| 4x-8x optic | 8x-12x optic |
| Squad role | 2 per team max |
| Support focus | Independent operation |
| Medium range | Long range |

## Responsibilities

1. **Provide overwatch** - Cover squad movements from distance
2. **Pick off key targets** - Enemy medics, MGs, AT
3. **Reconnaissance** - Spot and report enemy positions
4. **Suppress at range** - Keep enemy heads down
5. **Counter-sniper** - Engage enemy marksmen/snipers

## Tips for Success

### Target Priority
1. **Enemy HAT/LAT** - Protect your vehicles
2. **Enemy medics** - Prevent revives
3. **Machine gunners** - Remove suppression threats
4. **Squad Leaders** - Disrupt command
5. **Stationary targets** - Easy kills

### Positioning
- Stay **200-400m** from the fight
- Find elevated positions when possible
- Always have **concealment**
- Plan **escape routes**
- Don't skyline yourself

### Engagement Techniques
- **Hold breath** (Shift) for steady aim
- Lead moving targets appropriately
- Account for bullet drop at range
- Fire from **unexpected angles**
- Relocate after several shots

## Combat Tactics

### Squad Overwatch
1. Position ahead/flank of squad movement
2. Scan for enemies and report
3. Engage targets of opportunity
4. Call out enemy positions
5. Shift position as squad advances

### Defensive Operations
- Set up overlooking likely approaches
- Engage enemies at maximum range
- Force enemies to seek cover early
- Coordinate with MGs for kill zones

### Urban Combat
- Windows and rooftops are your friend
- Clear sightlines down streets
- Watch for enemy snipers/marksmen
- Be ready to relocate frequently

## Common Mistakes

- Getting too close to the fight
- Tunnel vision on one area
- Taking too many shots from one position
- Forgetting to communicate
- Lone wolfing away from the squad

## Ranging Your Shots

### Mil-Dot Ranging
Most DMRs use mil-dot or similar reticles:
- Use rangefinder marks on scope
- Known target sizes help estimate
- Infantry = ~1.8m standing

### Drop Compensation
| Range | Approximate Drop |
|-------|------------------|
| 200m | Minimal |
| 400m | First mil-dot below center |
| 600m | Second mil-dot |
| 800m+ | Significant holdover |

## Working with Your Squad

You are NOT a lone wolf:
- Stay in **communication range**
- Report everything you see
- Support squad objectives
- Rejoin for major pushes
- Don't chase kills across the map
