# Pilot

The Pilot is **required to fly helicopters** in Squad. Helicopters are crucial for rapid troop transport and logistics, making pilots essential team members.

## Role Limitations

- No squad limits
- Required for: All helicopter pilot/co-pilot seats
- Not required for: Helicopter passenger seats

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- Compact PDW or pistol
- Smoke grenade
- Field dressing
- Binoculars

Limited combat capability - your value is in flying.

## Responsibilities

1. **Transport troops** - Rapid deployment
2. **Move supplies** - Logistics runs
3. **Stay alive** - Helicopters are valuable
4. **Communicate** - Landing zones, pickup requests
5. **Support the team** - You serve infantry

## Tips for Success

### Pre-Flight
1. Check fuel levels
2. Verify passengers ready
3. Confirm landing zone with Squad Leaders
4. Plan your route (avoid enemy AA)
5. Communicate departure

### Flying Basics
- Collective (Space/Ctrl) for altitude
- Cyclic (WASD) for direction
- Tail rotor (A/D with no cyclic) for turning
- **Practice on Jensen's Range first!**

### Landing
- Scout LZ before committing
- Approach low and fast, then flare
- Communicate "30 seconds" and "Landing now"
- Don't hover - you're a target
- Get out fast

## Combat Flying

### Avoiding Enemy Fire
- Stay low (terrain masking)
- Keep moving
- Don't hover
- Use terrain features
- Vary your routes

### Taking Fire
- Pop smoke (some helicopters)
- Dive for terrain cover
- Call out damage
- Emergency land if critical
- Don't try to fight back (transport helis)

### Crash Procedures
- If going down, try to autorotate
- Aim for flat ground
- Call "Mayday" with location
- Survive if possible
- Stay with wreck for pickup

## Logistics Flying

### Supply Runs
1. Land at main base
2. Load supplies from logistics area
3. Fly to FOB location
4. Land and unload
5. Return for more

### Efficiency Tips
- Plan routes to serve multiple FOBs
- Coordinate with Squad Leaders
- Avoid hot zones
- Full loads only (worth the risk)

## Communication

Essential callouts:

| Call | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| "LZ hot" | Enemy at landing zone |
| "LZ cold" | Clear to land |
| "30 seconds" | Approaching LZ |
| "Touchdown" | On the ground |
| "Dusting off" | Taking off |
| "Winchester" | Out of ammo (attack heli) |

## Common Mistakes

- Not practicing before live games
- Hovering over combat zones
- Flying predictable routes
- Ignoring fuel levels
- Not communicating with passengers
- Crashing into terrain/wires

## Helicopter Ticket Values

| Helicopter | Approximate Tickets |
|------------|---------------------|
| Transport Heli | 5-10 tickets |
| Attack Heli | 10-15 tickets |

Plus all passengers if you crash - fly carefully!

## Learning to Fly

### Jensen's Range Practice
- Takeoff and landing
- Hovering (hardest skill)
- Autorotation
- Navigation
- Combat maneuvers

### Start Simple
1. Master hovering first
2. Then takeoff/landing
3. Forward flight
4. Finally combat flying

Helicopters have a steep learning curve - expect to crash a lot while learning. **Never practice in live games.**
