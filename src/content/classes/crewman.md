# Crewman

The Crewman is **required to operate heavy armored vehicles** like tanks and IFVs. Without this kit, you cannot enter the driver or gunner seats of most combat vehicles.

## Role Limitations

- No squad limits
- Required for: MBTs, IFVs, some APCs
- Not required for: Logistics, transports, helicopters

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- Compact PDW or pistol
- Repair wrench (limited repairs)
- 1x Smoke grenade
- Field dressing
- Binoculars

### Repair Wrench vs Repair Kit
| Repair Wrench (Crewman) | Repair Kit (Engineer) |
|------------------------|----------------------|
| Repairs to 25% max | Repairs to 100% |
| Quick field fixes | Full restoration |
| Limited effectiveness | Unlimited repairs |
| Always have it | Need Engineer kit |

## Vehicles Requiring Crewman

### Crewman Required
- Main Battle Tanks (MBTs)
- Infantry Fighting Vehicles (IFVs)
- Some APCs (faction-dependent)
- Recon vehicles

### Crewman NOT Required
- Logistics trucks
- Transport trucks
- Technical vehicles
- Most helicopters
- Scout cars

## Responsibilities

1. **Operate armored vehicles** - Drive or gun
2. **Field repair your vehicle** - Quick fixes
3. **Coordinate with crew** - Communication essential
4. **Protect your asset** - Don't waste tickets
5. **Support infantry** - You serve them

## Tips for Success

### Before Taking Armor
1. **Have a full crew** - Driver + Gunner minimum
2. **Communicate** - Discord/in-game voice
3. **Know the vehicle** - Practice on Jensen's Range
4. **Claim the vehicle** - Prevent theft
5. **Check supplies** - Ammo, fuel

### Driving Tips
- Stay on roads when possible
- Avoid steep terrain
- Keep moving in combat
- Position for gunner shots
- Communicate intentions

### Gunning Tips
- Communicate target acquisitions
- Use correct ammo (AP vs HE)
- Don't fire while moving (less accurate)
- Check for friendlies before firing
- Manage your ammunition

## The Repair Wrench

Your wrench is limited but useful:
- Quick track/wheel repair
- Stop light fires
- Emergency field repairs
- Buys time for Engineer

### When to Repair
- Engine damaged (can't move well)
- Tracks destroyed (immobilized)
- Light fire starting
- Hull damaged below 25%

### When to Fall Back
- Heavy fire (can't repair)
- Out of ammo
- Multiple components damaged
- Need full repair (find Engineer/repair station)

## Vehicle Communication

Standard callouts:
| Call | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| "Contact front" | Enemy ahead |
| "Backing up" | Reversing |
| "Popping smoke" | Deploying smoke |
| "Loading AP/HE" | Ammo type change |
| "Track hit" | Mobility damaged |
| "Bailing" | Abandoning vehicle |

## Common Mistakes

- Soloing vehicles (no crew coordination)
- Overextending into enemy lines
- Ignoring infantry support calls
- Forgetting to repair
- Not communicating with driver/gunner

## Crewman Ethics

### Do
- Wait for a full crew
- Stay with your vehicle
- Coordinate with command
- Support infantry objectives
- Return for repairs/resupply

### Don't
- Abandon your vehicle
- Lone wolf with armor
- Ignore Squad Leader requests
- Chase kills across the map
- Forget you're supporting infantry

## Vehicle Ticket Values

Losing vehicles costs tickets:

| Vehicle Type | Approximate Tickets |
|--------------|---------------------|
| MBT | 15-20 tickets |
| IFV | 10-15 tickets |
| APC | 5-10 tickets |

Protect your asset - you're responsible for significant team resources.
