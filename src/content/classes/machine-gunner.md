# Machine Gunner

The Machine Gunner carries a **general-purpose machine gun (GPMG)** - heavier and more powerful than the Automatic Rifleman's LMG. This role is the king of suppression.

## Role Limitations

- **Fire Support** role type
- Max **2 Machine Gunners** per team
- Also counts toward 3 Fire Support per squad

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- General Purpose Machine Gun (M240B, PKM, L7A2, etc.)
- 100-150 round belt
- Deployable bipod (essential)
- 1x Smoke grenade
- Field dressing

### Weapon Characteristics
| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Caliber** | 7.62x51mm NATO / 7.62x54R |
| **Fire rate** | 650-950 RPM |
| **Belt size** | 100-150 rounds |
| **Damage** | Higher than 5.56mm LMGs |
| **Recoil** | Significant without bipod |

## GPMG vs LMG

| GPMG (Machine Gunner) | LMG (Automatic Rifleman) |
|----------------------|-------------------------|
| 7.62mm caliber | 5.56mm caliber |
| More damage per hit | Less damage per hit |
| Heavier, slower | Lighter, more mobile |
| Longer effective range | Shorter effective range |
| Requires bipod for accuracy | More usable without bipod |

## Responsibilities

1. **Provide heavy suppression** - Your fire is terrifying
2. **Anchor defensive positions** - GPMGs hold ground
3. **Support assaults from distance** - Set up and cover
4. **Deny vehicle movement** - Can damage light vehicles
5. **Create kill zones** - Control engagement areas

## Tips for Success

### Always Deploy Bipod
- **Never fire standing** - You won't hit anything
- Find walls, windows, rocks, anything to deploy on
- Prone is most stable but limits visibility
- Crouch on low cover for better angles

### Positioning is Everything
- You are **NOT mobile** - choose positions carefully
- Stay **100-200m behind** the assault
- Find positions covering **long sight lines**
- Have an **escape route** planned

### Ammunition Management
- You burn ammo fast
- Stay near **Riflemen** and ammo sources
- Reload during lulls, not when dry
- Call for resupply proactively

## Combat Tactics

### Overwatch Position
1. Find elevated or covered position
2. Set up with good field of fire
3. Wait for squad to engage
4. Suppress enemy positions
5. Only move when squad is set

### Defensive Machine Gun Nest
- Pre-plan your firing lanes
- Have alternate positions ready
- Communicate with other MGs for interlocking fire
- Don't give away position until enemies commit

### Vehicle Harassment
- GPMGs can damage:
  - Logistics trucks
  - Transport vehicles
  - Helicopters
  - Light technicals
- Aim for engine blocks and tires

## Common Mistakes

- Moving too much (you're not assault)
- Firing without bipod
- Exposing yourself for kills
- Ignoring ammo management
- Setting up with no cover

## Advanced Tips

### Beaten Zone
The area where your rounds land naturally spreads. Use this:
- Aim at cover edges to catch movement
- Traverse slowly across enemy positions
- Let the spread work for you

### Tracer Discipline
- Your tracers reveal your position
- Use short bursts, relocate after long engagements
- Tracers can be used to direct squad fire

### Working with Other MGs
- Set up **interlocking fields of fire**
- Cover each other's reload times
- Communicate targets
