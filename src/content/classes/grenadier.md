# Grenadier

The Grenadier carries a rifle with an **underbarrel grenade launcher (UGL)**, providing the squad with portable indirect fire capability. Perfect for clearing buildings and hitting enemies in cover.

## Role Limitations

- **Fire Support** role type
- Max **3 Fire Support roles** per squad
- Shares pool with: Automatic Rifleman, Machine Gunner, Marksman

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- Assault rifle with UGL (M4+M203, AK-74+GP-25, etc.)
- Iron sights or red dot
- 8-12 HE grenades (40mm)
- 2-4 Smoke grenades (40mm)
- 1x Fragmentation grenade
- Field dressing

### Grenade Types
| Type | Use |
|------|-----|
| **HE (High Explosive)** | Killing infantry, destroying light cover |
| **Smoke** | Concealment for movement/revives |

## Grenade Launcher Mechanics

### Arming Distance
- Grenades have a **minimum arming distance** (~15-30m)
- Too close = dud (no explosion)
- Plan your engagement range

### Trajectory
- Grenades arc significantly
- Use the ladder sight for range estimation
- Practice on Jensen's Range

### Damage
- Direct hit = instant kill
- Splash damage in ~5m radius
- Can kill multiple enemies
- Destroys razor wire and sandbags

## Responsibilities

1. **Clear defended positions** - Flush enemies from cover
2. **Breach buildings** - Grenades through windows
3. **Smoke for the squad** - Larger smoke than hand grenades
4. **Area denial** - Keep enemies moving
5. **Destroy deployables** - Razor wire, sandbags, HESCOs

## Tips for Success

### Using the Sight
Most UGLs have a ladder sight:
- 50m, 100m, 150m, 200m, 250m marks
- Aim with the correct range line
- Account for elevation differences
- Practice makes perfect

### Target Priority
1. **Groups of infantry** - Maximum casualties
2. **Machine gun positions** - Suppress the suppressors
3. **Windows with enemies** - Clear buildings
4. **Enemies in cover** - Your grenades ignore their cover

### Ammunition Conservation
- You have limited grenades
- Don't waste on single targets
- Save smoke for critical moments
- Resupply from Riflemen/ammo crates

## Combat Tactics

### Building Clearance
1. Identify occupied windows
2. Fire HE through windows
3. Follow up with squad entry
4. Save grenades for stubborn defenders

### Supporting Assault
1. Stay slightly behind the assault
2. Watch for enemies in cover
3. Lob grenades over obstacles
4. Use smoke to cover final approach

### Defensive Use
- Pre-range likely approach routes
- Drop grenades on advancing enemies
- Create smoke screens when falling back
- Save some ammo for emergencies

## Common Mistakes

- Wasting grenades on single targets
- Firing too close (duds)
- Forgetting you still have a rifle
- Not using smoke
- Standing in the open to aim

## Range Estimation Tips

### Quick Reference
| Distance | Drop Compensation |
|----------|-------------------|
| 50m | First sight line |
| 100m | Second sight line |
| 150m | Third sight line |
| 200m+ | Top of sight |

### Environmental Factors
- Uphill = aim lower than normal
- Downhill = aim higher than normal
- Moving targets = lead significantly
