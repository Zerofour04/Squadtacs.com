# Squad Leader

The Squad Leader (SL) is the most important role in Squad. Every squad needs one, and good Squad Leaders win games.

## Responsibilities

1. **Create and manage the squad** - Name it, set it public/private
2. **Place Rally Points** - Mobile spawn for your squad
3. **Build FOBs and HABs** - Coordinate with other SLs for placement
4. **Communicate with Command** - Use the command channel (G key)
5. **Mark the map** - Place markers for enemies, objectives, and movement
6. **Lead your squad** - Give clear orders and maintain cohesion

## Equipment

- Standard rifle with optic (faction-dependent)
- Binoculars
- Smoke grenades (colored for marking)
- Rally Point
- Fortification building hammer

## Key Abilities

### Rally Point
- Costs **50 ammo** from a nearby ammo source (or no cost near a radio)
- Requires **2 squad members** within 8m to place
- **60 second** cooldown
- Destroyed if enemies come within **30m**
- Limited spawns before it needs replacing

### FOB Placement
- Requires **Radio** placement first
- Needs a logistics vehicle with **construction supplies**
- Radio has **300m exclusion zone** from other radios
- Can build HAB, ammo crates, fortifications, emplacements

## Communication Channels

| Key | Channel | Use |
|-----|---------|-----|
| B | Local | Nearby players |
| V | Squad | Your squad only |
| G | Command | All Squad Leaders + Commander |
| Numpad | Direct | Specific squad (hold number) |

## Tips for Success

### Start of Round
1. Name your squad clearly (INF 1, ARMOR, HELI, etc.)
2. Wait for 2 medics before moving out
3. Coordinate with other SLs on the plan
4. Claim vehicles if needed

### During the Game
- **Keep your squad together** - Lone wolves lose games
- **Always have an active rally** - Your squad's lifeline
- **Mark enemies constantly** - Information wins battles
- **Don't lone wolf** - You have the rally, stay alive

### Common Mistakes
- Forgetting to place rallies
- Not communicating with command channel
- Running into combat first (you're the most valuable)
- Building FOBs in obvious locations
- Not delegating tasks to Fireteam Leads

## Fireteam Management

Press **T** to open the Fireteam menu:
- Create up to 2 Fireteams (Alpha, Bravo)
- Assign Fireteam Leads who can place move markers
- Useful for splitting the squad tactically
