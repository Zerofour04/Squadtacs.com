# Medic

The Medic is one of the most important roles in Squad. Every squad needs **two medics** for sustained combat effectiveness. A good medic keeps the entire squad in the fight.

## Role Limitations

- **Standard** role type
- **2 Medics per squad maximum**
- Always take both slots

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- Standard assault rifle (iron sights)
- Medic bag (unlimited heals)
- Field dressings (6-8)
- Smoke grenades (2-4)
- Fragmentation grenade (sometimes)

### Medical Equipment
| Item | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Medic Bag** | Heals any wound to full HP |
| **Field Dressing** | Self-bandage or quick bandage others |
| **Smoke Grenade** | Cover for revives |

## The Revive System

### Downed vs Dead
- **Incapacitated**: Can be revived (bleedout timer ~5 min)
- **Dead**: Must respawn (give up or bleedout complete)

### Revive Process
1. Approach downed teammate
2. Hold F to begin revive
3. Revive takes ~7 seconds
4. Player wakes with low HP
5. Heal them immediately after

### Revive Rules
- **Anyone** can revive with bandages
- **Medics** revive faster
- **Medics** restore more HP on revive
- Revived players need **healing** after

## Responsibilities

1. **Revive downed teammates** - Your primary job
2. **Heal wounded players** - Keep health topped up
3. **Stay alive** - A dead medic helps no one
4. **Use smoke effectively** - Enable safe revives
5. **Prioritize revives** - Not all are equal

## Tips for Success

### Revive Priority
1. **Squad Leader** - Has the rally
2. **LAT/HAT** - Anti-vehicle capability
3. **Other Medic** - Doubles revive capacity
4. **Machine Gunners** - Suppression
5. **Everyone else**

### Smart Positioning
- Stay **10-20m behind** the assault
- Don't be first through doors
- Keep line of sight to your squad
- Have cover ready nearby

### Smoke Usage
- Throw smoke **between you and the enemy**
- Wait 2-3 seconds for smoke to bloom
- Move through smoke, don't stop in it
- Have an exit plan

## Combat Tactics

### Mass Casualty Situation
1. Find cover yourself first
2. Assess who can be saved safely
3. Use smoke liberally
4. Prioritize high-value revives
5. Don't die trying to save everyone

### Defensive Play
- Stay near cover at all times
- Call for fire support before moving
- Ask teammates to cover your revives
- Don't chase bodies into danger

### When NOT to Revive
- Enemy has sight on the body
- You'd die attempting it
- Better to wait for area secure
- Player can respawn on nearby rally

## Common Mistakes

- **Tunnel vision revives** - Running into fire to save one person
- **Ignoring heals** - Revived players need HP
- **Lone wolfing** - Medics should never be alone
- **No smoke usage** - Your best tool
- **Being first in combat** - Stay back

## Healing After Revive

Revived players spawn with ~35% HP:
- **Heal immediately** after revive
- Standing heal takes ~10 seconds to full
- They can still fight while healing
- Don't leave them wounded

## The Two-Medic Rule

Every squad should have two medics:
- One can cover the other's revive
- Double the revive capacity
- Insurance if one dies
- Sustained squad presence

If you see only one medic in your squad, **switch to medic**.

## Communication

Call these out:
- "Medic moving to [player]"
- "Popping smoke"
- "Need cover for revive"
- "Stay down, I'm coming"
- "[Player] is up, healing"

## Medic Mindset

Your kills don't matter. Your deaths matter a lot.

The best medics have:
- Few kills
- Many revives
- Zero unnecessary deaths
- Happy squad members
