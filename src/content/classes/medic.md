# Combat Medic

The Medic is one of the most important roles in Squad. A good medic can turn the tide of battle by keeping their squad in the fight.

## Responsibilities

1. **Revive downed teammates** - Your primary job
2. **Heal wounded players** - Keep health topped up
3. **Stay alive** - A dead medic helps no one
4. **Position smartly** - Don't rush into danger

## Equipment

- Standard rifle (usually without optics)
- Medic bag (unlimited heals)
- Field dressings
- Smoke grenades (essential for revives)

## Tips for Success

### Prioritize Your Revives
Not all revives are equal. Consider:
- Is it safe to revive?
- Is the downed player in a useful position?
- Are there other medics closer?

### Use Smoke Effectively
- Throw smoke BETWEEN you and the enemy
- Wait for smoke to fully deploy before moving
- Have an exit plan

### Stay Behind the Frontline
Your job isn't to get kills. Stay 10-20m behind the assault element so you can:
- See who goes down
- Respond quickly
- Avoid getting shot first

## Common Mistakes

- **Rushing revives**: Getting killed trying to save one person
- **Ignoring heals**: Revived players need healing too
- **Lone wolfing**: Medics should never be alone
