# Combat Engineer

The Combat Engineer is the demolitions and fortification expert, carrying **C4 explosives**, repair tools, and anti-personnel mines. This role is crucial for destroying enemy FOBs and repairing vehicles.

## Role Limitations

- **Specialist** role type
- **2 Combat Engineers per team maximum**
- Essential for FOB hunting

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- Compact rifle or shotgun
- C4 explosive charge (1-2)
- Anti-personnel mine (1-2)
- Repair toolkit
- Sandbags/fortification materials
- Field dressing

### Equipment Details
| Item | Use |
|------|-----|
| **C4 Explosive** | Destroys radios, vehicles, buildings |
| **AP Mine** | Kills infantry who step on it |
| **Repair Kit** | Fixes damaged vehicles |
| **Sandbags** | Quick fortification |

## C4 Explosives

### Damage Output
- **1 C4** = Destroys radio (instant FOB kill)
- **1 C4** = Destroys most light vehicles
- **2 C4** = Destroys APCs/IFVs
- **2-3 C4** = Destroys MBTs

### Placement Tips
- C4 sticks to surfaces
- Place directly on target when possible
- Can be shot by enemies to disarm
- 10-second fuse after detonation

### Detonation
1. Place C4 (left click)
2. Back away (at least 15m)
3. Equip detonator
4. Trigger explosion (left click)

## Anti-Personnel Mines

### Mechanics
- Buried and nearly invisible
- Triggers when enemy steps on it
- Kills or severely wounds infantry
- Shows on friendly map
- Can teamkill - be careful!

### Best Placements
- Paths near enemy FOBs
- Chokepoints in buildings
- Near objectives
- Vehicle paths (can damage wheels)

## Repair Toolkit

### Vehicle Repair
- Hold repair tool near damaged vehicle
- Progress bar shows repair
- Fully repairs to 100% (unlike crewman wrench)
- Can repair while vehicle is occupied
- Repairs all components (engine, tracks, etc.)

### Multiple Engineers
- 2 engineers repair faster
- Essential for heavily damaged vehicles
- Can out-repair fire damage together

## Responsibilities

1. **Destroy enemy FOBs** - Your C4 is the fastest way
2. **Repair friendly vehicles** - Keep armor in the fight
3. **Lay mines** - Deny enemy movement
4. **Build fortifications** - Quick defensive positions
5. **Breach buildings** - C4 can make new entrances

## Tips for Success

### FOB Hunting
1. Locate enemy radio (listen for static)
2. Approach carefully (enemies nearby)
3. Place C4 directly on radio
4. Back away and detonate
5. FOB destroyed instantly

### Vehicle Repair Priority
1. MBTs (most valuable)
2. IFVs (high value)
3. APCs (transport capability)
4. Logistics (essential for supplies)

### Mine Tactics
- Don't mine obvious spots (enemies expect it)
- Use near enemy HABs for spawning enemies
- Combine with good sightlines
- Mark mines on map (right-click)

## Combat Tactics

### FOB Assault
1. Scout radio location
2. Clear immediate enemies
3. Plant C4 on radio
4. Cover while detonating
5. Confirm destruction

### Vehicle Support
- Stay near friendly armor
- Repair during lulls in combat
- Call for covering fire during repairs
- Prioritize mobility (tracks/wheels first)

### Defensive Engineering
- Build sandbag positions quickly
- Mine approaches to your FOB
- Create chokepoints
- Block vehicle paths

## Common Mistakes

- Using C4 on infantry (waste)
- Mining friendly routes (teamkills)
- Dying with C4 near enemies (wasted)
- Not communicating mine locations
- Forgetting repair kit exists

## Combat Engineer vs Crewman

| Combat Engineer | Crewman |
|-----------------|---------|
| Full repair toolkit | Repair wrench (partial) |
| C4 explosives | No explosives |
| Can't drive most vehicles | Required for armor |
| Infantry focus | Vehicle focus |

## The FOB Killer

Your most important job is destroying enemy FOBs:
- One C4 on the radio = entire FOB gone
- Radio destroyed = HAB disabled
- All spawns stopped
- All fortifications gone

Find radios, destroy radios, win games.
