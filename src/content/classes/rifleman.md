# Rifleman

The Rifleman is the backbone of every squad. While it may seem like a "basic" role, the Rifleman's **ammo bag** makes them essential for sustained combat operations.

## Why Rifleman Matters

Every squad should have **at least one Rifleman** because:
- **Ammo bag** provides 100 ammo points anywhere on the map
- No special kit requirements
- Unlimited slots available
- Perfect for learning the game

## Equipment

### Standard Loadout
- Assault rifle (M4, AK-74, L85A2, etc.)
- Iron sights or red dot (faction-dependent)
- 2x Fragmentation grenades
- 2x Smoke grenades (white)
- Field dressing
- **Ammo bag** (100 points)

### Optic Variant
Some factions have a Rifleman variant with magnified optics:
- 4x ACOG or similar scope
- Slightly reduced grenade count
- Same ammo bag

## The Ammo Bag

The ammo bag is the Rifleman's superpower:

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Capacity** | 100 ammo points |
| **Rearm cost** | Varies by item (rockets ~40, bandage ~5) |
| **Placement** | Drop with right-click when selected |
| **Pickup** | Can be picked up and moved |
| **Resupply** | Refill at FOB ammo crate or vehicle |

### What It Can Resupply
- LAT/HAT rockets
- Grenades
- Bandages
- Ammo magazines
- Throwables

## Responsibilities

1. **Stay near your squad** - Your ammo bag is useless if you're alone
2. **Drop ammo proactively** - Don't wait to be asked
3. **Support the specialists** - LAT/HAT need your ammo most
4. **Provide covering fire** - You're expendable, specialists aren't
5. **Learn the game** - Perfect role for beginners

## Tips for Success

### Ammo Management
- **Ask who needs ammo** after every engagement
- Drop the bag in **safe cover**, not in the open
- Stay near your **LAT/HAT** players
- Resupply your bag whenever near an ammo source

### Combat Tips
- Use your grenades for clearing buildings
- Smoke is for covering revives and movement
- Don't be afraid to trade your life for a specialist's survival
- Stick with your Fireteam

### Supporting Your Squad
- Call out enemies you see
- Follow your Squad Leader's orders
- Volunteer for dangerous tasks
- Keep count of nearby enemies

## Common Mistakes

- Forgetting you have an ammo bag
- Running off alone
- Using all grenades immediately
- Not resupplying the bag when possible
- Trying to be a "hero" instead of supporting

## Best Practices

### Start of Round
1. Spawn with Rifleman (quick spawn, no wait)
2. Drop ammo for LAT/HAT to grab extra rockets
3. Stay close to your squad

### During Combat
1. Find cover first
2. Support fire for assaulting teammates
3. Drop ammo when requested
4. Save smokes for medic revives

### Recommended for
- New players learning Squad
- Players who want to help without specialized knowledge
- Squads needing ammo resupply capability
