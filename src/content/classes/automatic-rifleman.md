# Automatic Rifleman

The Automatic Rifleman (AR) carries a light machine gun and provides **suppressive fire** to support squad movements. This role is about volume of fire, not precision.

## Role Limitations

- **Fire Support** role type
- Max **3 Fire Support roles** per squad
- Shares pool with: Machine Gunner, Grenadier, Marksman

## Equipment

### Typical Loadout
- Light Machine Gun (M249, RPK, L110A2, etc.)
- 200-round belt/drum magazines
- Deployable bipod (press C when prone/on cover)
- 1x Fragmentation grenade
- 1x Smoke grenade
- Field dressing

### Weapon Characteristics
| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Fire rate** | 700-900 RPM |
| **Magazine** | 45-200 rounds |
| **Bipod** | Yes (deploy with C) |
| **Optics** | Usually iron sights or 1x red dot |

## The Role of Suppression

Suppression in Squad:
- **Blurs enemy vision** when rounds land nearby
- **Sways their aim** making return fire difficult
- Gives your squad time to **maneuver**
- Forces enemies to **stay in cover**

## Responsibilities

1. **Suppress enemy positions** - Keep their heads down
2. **Support squad movement** - Fire while others advance
3. **Hold lanes** - Cover avenues of approach
4. **Deny area** - Make areas dangerous to cross
5. **Protect the squad** - React to contacts quickly

## Tips for Success

### Positioning
- **Always use your bipod** - Standing fire is inaccurate
- Find positions with **good sight lines**
- Stay **slightly behind** the assault element
- Set up on **flanks** when possible

### Fire Discipline
- **Short bursts** (5-10 rounds) for accuracy
- **Long bursts** (20+ rounds) for suppression
- Don't burn through all ammo immediately
- Watch your barrel heat (sustained fire reduces accuracy)

### Working with Your Squad
- Call out when you're **suppressing** a position
- Coordinate with Squad Leader for fire and movement
- Let others know when you're **reloading**
- Stay near a **Rifleman** for ammo resupply

## Combat Tactics

### Bounding Overwatch
1. Set up in a covered position
2. Suppress enemy while squad moves
3. Squad reaches cover and returns fire
4. You move up while they suppress
5. Repeat

### Defensive Positions
- Set up on likely enemy approach routes
- Pre-aim at chokepoints
- Wait for multiple targets before engaging
- Conserve ammo for sustained defense

### Urban Combat
- Cover streets and alleyways
- Suppress windows while squad enters buildings
- Watch for flankers

## Common Mistakes

- Firing without bipod deployed
- Using all ammo in one engagement
- Not communicating suppression
- Staying in one position too long (counter-sniped)
- Forgetting you have grenades

## Compared to Machine Gunner

|-------------------|----------------|
