## Overview

The **Loach** (OH-6 Cayuse) is the WPMC light observation helicopter. Introduced in Version 8.1 "Hired Guns", it is the only close air support helicopter currently in Squad.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - Scout (unarmed) and CAS (armed)
- **Only CAS helo** - Miniguns + rockets
- **Extremely fast** - Most agile helicopter
- **Very fragile** - Engine disabled by rifles
- **Pilot vulnerable** - Can be shot out
- **WPMC exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **Cannot place Radio** - No FOB capability
- **Cannot carry build** - No construction supplies
- **Full kit selection** - Can resupply kits
- **Rockets auto-fire** - Hold LMB
- **Low ammo** - Only 7+7 rockets, rearm often
- **Analog gauges** - Not digital displays
- **Speed is armor** - Rely on agility to survive

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **CAS = hit and run** - Don't hover, keep moving
2. **Scout = transport** - Fast insertion/extraction
3. **Rockets limited** - 14 total, use carefully
4. **Rearm frequently** - Only 50 ammo points (CAS)
5. **Avoid small arms** - Engine dies to rifles
6. **Use terrain** - Nap-of-earth flying
7. **Don't hover** - Stationary = dead
8. **Pilot exposed** - Can be headshot
