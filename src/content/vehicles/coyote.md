## Overview

The **Coyote Reconnaissance Vehicle** is a Canadian 8×8 recon platform based on the LAV chassis. Introduced in V3.0 for Canadian Army, it's the predecessor to the LAV III and LAV 6.0 programs.

**Key Features:**

- **25mm M242 Bushmaster** - 75 AP + 135 HE
- **C6A1 FLEX commander MG** - 360° self-defense (2500 rds)
- **No passengers** - Pure recon, no transport
- **Non-amphibious** - Unlike LAV-25/ASLAV-25
- **100 km/h** - Fast wheeled platform
- **CAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **Recon role** - No passengers, pure combat
- **Same 25mm as LAV** - Effective vs IFVs
- **Non-amphibious** - Extra fuel tanks replace swim gear
- **Commander optics** lack stabilization
- **C6A1 FLEX** - 2500 rds for 360° coverage
- Similar resemblance to LAV-25
- Predecessor to LAV III and LAV 6.0

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Flanking recon** - Speed for repositioning
2. **Commander MG** - Constant 360° awareness
3. **Not amphibious** - Plan routes accordingly
4. **25mm AP** - Effective vs light armor
5. **No transport** - Don't expect passengers
6. **Pair with infantry** - No dismounts
7. **1250 HP** - Moderate survivability
