# Coyote

The **Coyote** is a reconnaissance vehicle used by the Canadian Armed Forces in Squad. Based on the LAV 6.0 chassis, it trades passenger capacity for additional fuel tanks and reconnaissance equipment.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Fast wheeled reconnaissance platform
- Same firepower as LAV 6.0
- Extended range with additional fuel tanks
- Commander has separate MG for self-defense

**Weaknesses:**
- Not amphibious
- No passenger capacity
- Commander optics not stabilized (unlike LAV 6.0)
- Slightly less HE ammo than LAV 6.0

---

## Tactical Notes

- The Coyote is designed for reconnaissance, not troop transport
- Use it for flanking maneuvers and scouting enemy positions
- The commander's C6A1 FLEX provides 360° coverage for self-defense
- Nearly identical to LAV 6.0 in combat capability
- Best used in pairs or with infantry support
