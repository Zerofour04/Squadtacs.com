## Overview

The MRH-90 Taipan is the Australian variant of the NH Industries NH90. It serves as the primary transport helicopter for the ADF, offering high passenger capacity and door gun support.

**Key Feature**: Higher passenger capacity than the UH-60M (16 vs 11).

## Weapons Systems

### Mag58 Door Guns (x2)
- 7.62mm belt-fed machine guns
- 1000 rounds per gun
- Effective for suppressing LZs during insertion
- Limited traverse angles

## Flight Characteristics

- **Cruise Speed**: Moderate
- **Maneuverability**: Good for its size
- **Durability**: Light armor, vulnerable to all weapons
- **Autorotation**: Possible if engine is disabled

---

## Tactical Tips

### For Pilots
1. **Fly low and fast** to avoid enemy fire
2. **Plan your approach** to the LZ beforehand
3. **Don't hover** over contested areas
4. **Use terrain masking** when possible
5. **Coordinate with door gunners** for suppression

### For Door Gunners
1. **Suppress the LZ** during approach
2. **Watch for RPGs and HAT** - call out threats
3. **Conserve ammo** for critical moments
4. **Lead moving targets** significantly

### For Passengers
1. **Exit quickly** - don't block the doors
2. **Move away from the LZ** immediately
3. **Don't shoot from inside** - it's ineffective
