## Overview

The **MRH-90 Taipan** is the Australian designation for the European NH90 TTH. Introduced in V2.15, it serves as ADF's primary transport helicopter with 1500 supply capacity.

**Key Features:**

- **1500 supply capacity** - Same as Mi-8, Z-8
- **Twin M240H door guns** - 5x200 rounds each
- **European design** - NH Industries NH90
- **Rear ramp** - Quick troop exit
- **Fly-by-wire** - Modern flight controls
- **ADF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **1500 supply** - Efficient FOB resupply
- **Rear ramp** - Fast troop exit
- **Door guns** - LZ suppression
- **No armor** - Vulnerable to all weapons
- **Modern design** - Good handling
- **ADF exclusive** - Australian faction only

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **1500 supply** - Fewer trips than UH-60M
2. **Use rear ramp** - Faster dismount
3. **Door guns suppress** - Cover landing zone
4. **Fly low and fast** - Minimize exposure
5. **Don't hover** - Keep moving
6. **Terrain masking** - Use hills/buildings
7. **Call out threats** - Gunners spot RPGs
8. **Quick extract** - Don't linger on ground
