## Overview

The **ZSL92** (WZ-551) is a 6x6 wheeled amphibious IFV/APC family serving the PLA. Introduced in Squad v10.3, it's available on PLA Combined Arms and Motorized units. The vehicle features a fully modeled interior.

**Key Features:**

- **3 variants** - QJZ89 HMG, QLZ87 grenade launcher, 25mm IFV
- **6x6 wheeled** - Road-mobile, amphibious
- **Fully modeled interior** - Detailed inside view
- **ZSL92 IFV has 25mm** - Plus 7.62mm coaxial MG
- **25mm has NO stabilizer** - Stop to shoot accurately
- **Lower turret rotation** than BLUFOR IFVs

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Multiple variants for different roles
- 6x6 wheeled mobility for roads
- Amphibious capability
- QLZ87 excellent for area denial
- ZSL92 IFV can engage light armor
- Fully modeled interior

**Weaknesses:**
- Open-top APC variants expose gunner
- Lower turret rotation than BLUFOR IFVs
- 25mm has NO stabilizer
- Amphibious but slow in water
