## Overview

The **SA330 Puma** is a French-British medium transport helicopter. Introduced in V2.0, it serves as the BAF's primary transport helicopter with 1200 supply capacity and retractable landing gear.

**Key Features:**

- **1200 supply capacity** - 300 less than Mi-8
- **Twin M240H door guns** - 5x200 rounds each
- **Retractable landing gear** - First helicopter with this feature
- **9 passengers** - Plus 4 crew
- **BAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **1200 supply** - Moderate capacity
- **Retractable gear** - Visual indicator of altitude
- **Door guns** - LZ suppression
- **No armor** - Vulnerable to all weapons
- **BAF exclusive** - British faction only

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **1200 supply** - More trips than Mi-8/Z-8
2. **Landing gear** - Auto-retracts at ~15m
3. **Door guns** - Suppress landing zone
4. **Fly low and fast** - Minimize exposure
5. **Don't hover** - Keep moving
6. **Terrain masking** - Use hills/buildings
7. **Call out threats** - Gunners spot RPGs
8. **Quick extract** - Don't linger on ground
