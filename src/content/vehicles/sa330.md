# SA330 Puma

The SA330 Puma is the British Army's primary transport helicopter in Squad, capable of rapidly deploying infantry across the battlefield.

## Specifications

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Tickets | 5 |
| Crew | 4 (Pilot, Co-Pilot, 2x Door Gunners) |
| Passengers | 12 |
| Respawn | 6 min |
| Ammo Points | 1500 |

## Armament

- **Door Guns**: 2x L7A2 7.62mm GPMG

## Features

- Large passenger capacity
- Door gunners for suppression
- Can carry supplies
- Reliable and maneuverable

## Tactical Notes

The Puma excels at rapid troop insertion and logistics runs. Door gunners should suppress enemy positions during approach and departure. Avoid hovering in contested areas.
