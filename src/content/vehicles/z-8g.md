# Z-8G

The **Z-8G** (Changhe Z-18) is a triple-engine medium transport helicopter manufactured by Changhe Aircraft Industries Corporation (CAIC). Introduced in 2018 as a replacement for the Harbin Z-8, it is the largest helicopter in Squad with a total capacity of 27 personnel.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Largest helicopter in Squad (27 total capacity)
- 1500 supply capacity (same as Mi-8/Mi-17/MRH-90)
- Z-8J variant has 360° recon camera for aerial reconnaissance
- Can transport entire squads in one trip

**Weaknesses:**
- Only unarmed helicopter in Squad (no door guns)
- Large profile makes it easy to spot and hit
- Vulnerable to all anti-air weapons
- Triple-engine design is a large target

---

## Tactical Notes

- The Z-8G is the only completely unarmed helicopter in Squad - rely on speed and escorts
- Z-8J variant (PLANMC exclusive) has a 360° rotating recon camera for the copilot, similar to UH-1Y and CH-146
- Use the massive 27-person capacity to transport multiple squads for coordinated assaults
- The Z-8G was introduced in v4.0 as part of the Red Star Rising update
- Based on the French Aerospatiale SA 321 Super Frelon design via the Harbin Z-8
- Unique triple-engine arrangement: two engines on one side, one on the other (third engine powers tail rotor)
