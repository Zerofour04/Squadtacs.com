# CH-146 Griffon CAS

The **CH-146 Griffon CAS** (Close Air Support) is an attack variant of the Griffon helicopter used by the Canadian Armed Forces in Squad. It trades passenger capacity for heavy armament.

## Tactical Notes

- Dedicated close air support platform
- GAU-19 minigun devastates infantry and light vehicles
- Hydra rockets effective against armor and emplacements
- Requires coordination with ground forces for target marking
- More valuable than transport variant - use wisely
- Vulnerable to AA and heavy MG fire
