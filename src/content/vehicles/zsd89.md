## Overview

The **ZSD89** (Type 89) is an amphibious tracked armored personnel carrier serving PLA and PLAAGF. Introduced in Squad v10.3, it's available on PLA Armored and PLAAGF Amphibious Assault, Armored, Combined Arms, and Mechanized units.

**Key Features:**

- **3 variants** - QJZ89 (HMG), QLZ87 (grenade launcher), ZSD89II (25mm IFV)
- **Open-top turrets** - APC variants have exposed gunners
- **Amphibious** - Water-capable but not fast
- **Tracked mobility** - Better off-road than wheeled APCs
- **ZSD89II has 25mm** - Light IFV capability

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Multiple variants for different roles
- Amphibious capability
- QLZ87 variant has 35mm grenade launcher
- ZSD89II can engage light armor
- Tracked mobility for rough terrain

**Weaknesses:**
- Open-top APC variants expose gunner
- Lower turret rotation than BLUFOR IFVs
- ZSD89II 25mm has NO stabilizer
- Amphibious but slow in water

---

## Tactical Notes

- ZSD89 QJZ89 and QLZ87 are open-top APCs - protect gunner
- QLZ87 variant excellent for clearing buildings and suppressing movement
- ZSD89II IFV variant has 25mm autocannon but NO stabilizer - stop to shoot accurately
- Lower turret rotation speed than Western IFVs
- Amphibious capability allows water crossings
- Available on PLA Armored, PLAAGF Amphibious/Armored/Combined Arms/Mechanized
