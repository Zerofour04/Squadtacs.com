## Overview

The **Kozak-2M1** is a Ukrainian MRAP (Mine-Resistant Ambush Protected) vehicle. Introduced in Version 10.0 (Trident Strike) for Armed Forces of Ukraine, it serves as a protected transport with direct fire support capability.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - Kord HMG and AGS-17 AGL
- **MRAP design** - Mine/IED protection
- **High-speed transport** - Fast troop delivery
- **Light fire support** - Suppression capability
- **No crewman kit** required
- **AFU exclusive**
