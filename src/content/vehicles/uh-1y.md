## Overview

The UH-1Y Venom is the USMC's primary utility helicopter, a modernized descendant of the legendary UH-1 "Huey." It provides rapid troop transport and fire support for Marine expeditionary operations.

**Key Feature**: Iconic Marine helicopter with excellent maneuverability.

## Tactical Tips

### For Pilots

1. **Use terrain masking** - Fly low behind hills and tree lines
2. **Fast insertions** - Don't hover over LZ longer than necessary
3. **Multiple LZ options** - Have backup landing zones planned
4. **Coordinate with door gunners** - Bank to give them firing angles

### For Door Gunners

1. **Controlled bursts** - Don't waste all ammo in one pass
2. **Scan constantly** - Look for threats and targets
3. **Call out contacts** - Keep the pilot informed
4. **Suppress during landing** - Keep heads down as troops dismount
