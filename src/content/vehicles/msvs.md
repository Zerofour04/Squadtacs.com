## Overview

The **MSVS** (Medium Support Vehicle System) is a Canadian logistics truck. Introduced in B20, it replaced the MLVW trucks (in service since 1980s). Has the **highest passenger capacity** of all trucks in its class.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - Transport, Logistics
- **Highest passenger capacity** - 20 transport, 7 logistics
- **6x6 configuration** - Good off-road
- **6000 total supply** - Logistics (3000/3000)
- **No crewman kit** required
- **No armor/weapons** - Avoid combat
- **CAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **Highest capacity** - 20 passengers (Transport)
- **No armor** - Any weapon damages
- **No weapons** - Cannot defend
- **Exposed passengers** - Flatbed not protected
- **5 tickets** - Significant loss
- **6x6** - Good off-road capability
- **Avoid armor** - Retreat if engaged

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Initial deployment** - 20 troops at once
2. **Avoid combat** - No protection
3. **Use roads** - Faster transport
4. **Don't rush objectives** - With logistics variant
5. **Retreat if engaged** - By armor
6. **Logi runs** - Keep FOBs supplied
7. **Exposed flatbed** - Passengers vulnerable
8. **Coordinate with SL** - Supply priorities
