# MSVS Truck

The **MSVS** (Medium Support Vehicle System) is a logistics and transport truck used by the Canadian Armed Forces in Squad. It has the highest passenger capacity of all trucks in its class.

## Variants

### MSVS Transport
- Highest passenger capacity in class
- Best for moving large numbers of troops
- No supply carrying capability

### MSVS Logistics
- Reduced passenger capacity
- Can carry 3000 supply points
- Essential for FOB construction

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Highest passenger capacity of any truck
- Large supply capacity (Logistics variant)
- Good off-road capability (6x6)
- Essential for logistics operations

**Weaknesses:**
- No armor protection
- No weapons
- Passengers exposed in truck bed
- Vulnerable to any enemy fire

---

## Tactical Notes

- MSVS replaced the older MLVW trucks in 2018
- Transport variant is ideal for initial squad deployment
- Logistics variant is critical for FOB building
- Avoid direct combat - retreat if engaged by armor
- Passengers are directly exposed to enemy fire
- Available in 4x2, 4x4, 6x4, and 6x6 configurations (only 6x6 in Squad)
