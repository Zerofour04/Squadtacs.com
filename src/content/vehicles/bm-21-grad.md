## Overview

The **BM-21 Grad** ("Hail") is a Soviet-era self-propelled multiple rocket launcher (MRL). Introduced in V4.3 for the Irregular Militia Forces, it's the only true rocket artillery system in Squad.

**Key Features:**

- **40 rockets** in a single salvo
- **122mm HE-Frag** warheads
- **Area denial** capability
- **Highly visible** smoke trails reveal position
- **No armor** - soft-skinned truck

## Tactical Employment

### Positioning

1. **Stay near a FOB** - Reloading 40 rockets takes significant ammo
2. **Fire and relocate** - Smoke trails reveal your position instantly
3. **Use cover** - Soft-skinned, 750 HP means any AT kills you
4. **Indirect positions** - You don't need line of sight

### Target Selection

| Good Targets | Poor Targets |
|--------------|--------------|
| Infantry in open | Armored vehicles |
| FOBs/HABs | Moving targets |
| Defensive positions | Single enemies |
| Objectives | Scattered infantry |

---

## Gameplay Tips

1. **Coordinate with SL** for target designation
2. **Pre-plan firing positions** with escape routes
3. **One full salvo** = need FOB resupply
4. **Don't fire too often from same position** - you will be hunted
5. **300 ammo points** on board for emergency infantry resupply
6. **Devastating but vulnerable** - protect this asset

---

## Vulnerabilities

- **No armor** - any weapon can damage
- **Low HP (750)** - fragile for 10 tickets
- **Huge signature** - smoke trails visible across map
- **Slow reload** - one salvo then resupply
- **Static when firing** - easy target

---

## Historical Background

The BM-21 entered Soviet service in 1963, replacing the BM-14. Built on the Ural-375D 6×6 truck chassis, it carries 40 launch tubes in a rectangular array. The system became one of the most widely exported rocket artillery systems in history.
