## Overview

The **BMC-185** is the Turkish Land Forces' standard military truck. Introduced in V7.0, it comes in **Transport** and **Logistics** variants. Like all faction trucks, it's unarmored and vulnerable.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - Transport (more passengers) and Logistics (supplies)
- **No armor** - Small arms can disable engine/tires
- **TLF exclusive** - Introduced with Turkish faction

---

## Variants

| Variant | Role | Passengers | Supplies |
|---------|------|------------|----------|
| **Transport** | Troop movement | Many | None |
| **Logistics** | FOB supply | Few | Yes |

---

## Transport Variant

The Transport variant prioritizes passenger capacity:

- Best for moving large squads
- No supply carrying ability
- Low ticket cost

---

## Logistics Variant

The Logistics variant trades passengers for supplies:

- Carries ammo and construction points
- Essential for FOB building
- Use the radial menu to load/unload supplies

For supply mechanics, see the [Logistic System](/guides/logistics).

---

## Vulnerabilities

The BMC-185 has **no armor**:

- Engine disabled by small arms fire to front grille
- Tires can be shot out
- Any enemy can stop you
- Keep away from frontlines
- Use cover and concealed routes

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Stay behind the lines** - You're defenseless
2. **Use covered routes** for logistics runs
3. **Don't stop in the open** - Easy target
4. **Coordinate with armor** for protection
5. **Multiple logistics runs** beat one failed run
