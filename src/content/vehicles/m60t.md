## Overview

The **M60T Sabra** is an upgraded M60 Patton tank used by TLF and WPMC. Developed by Israel Military Industries, it features a modern 120mm cannon with gun-launched LAHAT ATGMs.

**Key Features:**

- **120mm MG253** - Israeli smoothbore cannon
- **LAHAT ATGMs** - 2 gun-launched missiles
- **M85 Commander MG** - Rare HMG (smaller M2 replacement)
- **Weak armor** - Trade-off for firepower
- **58 km/h forward and reverse** - Equal speed both ways
- **TLF + WPMC**

## Gameplay

- **Gun-launched ATGMs** - Engage at range first
- **Weak armor** - Avoid direct MBT duels
- **Equal forward/reverse** - Fast retreats
- **M85 commander** - Extra firepower, protected
- **Limited depression** - Only -1.5° at front
- **8s reload** - Slower than M1A2 (6.5s)

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use LAHAT first** - Engage at distance
2. **Avoid frontal duels** - Weak armor
3. **Exploit reverse speed** - 58 km/h both ways
4. **M85 for infantry** - Commander suppression
5. **Hull-down limited** - Poor frontal depression
6. **Flanking attacks** - Side shots effective
7. **Support role** - Not a frontline MBT
8. **Disable first** - Before they shoot you
