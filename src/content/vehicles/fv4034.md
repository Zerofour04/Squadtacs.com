## Overview

The **FV4034 Challenger 2** is the British main battle tank. Added in Alpha 14.0 for British Armed Forces, it features a unique 120mm rifled gun capable of firing APFSDS, HESH, and white phosphorus rounds.

**Key Features:**

- **120mm rifled gun** - Unique to British, fires HESH
- **4-man crew** - Driver, gunner, commander, loader
- **Hunter-killer** - Commander can designate targets
- **Remote loader MG** - L37A2 RWS, no crew exposed
- **3 periscopes** - Only MBT with gunner, commander, and loader optics
- **BAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **Rifled gun** - No in-game performance difference
- **HESH rounds** - Effective vs light armor and infantry
- **WP smoke rounds** - Block LOS or kill infantry on impact
- **Hunter-killer** - Commander can slew gunner to target
- **Remote loader MG** - Only MBT with this feature
- **Long range optimized** - Gun sight calibrated for distance
- **8s reload** - 7.5 RPM fire rate

| Area | Vulnerability |

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Hull-down** - Use excellent gun depression (-10°)
2. **Long range** - Gun sight optimized for distance
3. **HESH for infantry** - Also damages light vehicles
4. **WP rounds** - Smoke or anti-personnel
5. **Loader MG** - 12x zoom for infantry hunting
6. **Protect lower hull** - Major frontal weakness
7. **All crew safe** - No exposed positions like T-72
