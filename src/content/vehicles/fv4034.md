# FV4034 Challenger 2

The Challenger 2 is the British Army's main battle tank, known for its exceptional frontal armor and powerful 120mm rifled gun. It's one of the most heavily armored tanks in Squad.

## Specifications

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Tickets | 15 |
| Crew | 4 (Driver, Gunner, Commander, Loader) |
| HP | 3500 |
| Respawn | 20 min |

## Armament

- **Main Gun**: L30A1 120mm Rifled Gun (APFSDS + HESH)
- **Coaxial**: L94A1 7.62mm
- **Commander**: L37A2 7.62mm

## Features

- Excellent frontal armor protection
- Rifled gun allows use of HESH rounds
- Slower than other NATO MBTs
- Dorchester composite armor

## Tactical Notes

The Challenger 2 excels in defensive positions where its armor can be utilized. The rifled gun's HESH rounds are effective against light vehicles and fortifications.
