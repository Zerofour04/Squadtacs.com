## Overview

The Bushmaster PMV (Protected Mobility Vehicle) is the primary infantry transport for the Australian Defence Force. Designed for the harsh Australian outback, it features excellent mine protection and all-terrain capability.

---

## Variants

### PMV Mag58
- **Weapon**: Single Mag58 7.62mm machine gun (800 rounds)
- **Crew**: 2 (Driver + Gunner)
- **Passengers**: 9
- **Role**: Standard infantry transport

### PMV Mag58 x3
- **Weapon**: Three Mag58 7.62mm machine guns (800 rounds each)
- **Crew**: 4 (Driver + 3 Gunners)
- **Passengers**: 5
- **Role**: Fire support variant with increased firepower

### PMV M2 RWS
- **Weapon**: M2A1 .50 Cal Remote Weapon Station (400 rounds)
- **Crew**: 2 (Driver + RWS Operator)
- **Passengers**: 9
- **Ammo Points**: 400
- **Role**: Heavy fire support, gunner operates from inside vehicle

---

## Armor & Protection

The Bushmaster features STANAG 4569 Level 1-3 protection:
- **V-hull design** for excellent mine/IED protection
- **All-round protection** against small arms and fragments
- **Vulnerable** to heavy weapons and AT systems
- **Best used** for transport rather than direct combat

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Don't engage armor** - you will lose
2. **Transport infantry quickly** to objectives
3. **Use the x3 variant** for suppressive fire support
4. **RWS variant** allows safer engagement from inside
5. **Drop troops** before entering contested areas
6. **Stay mobile** - a stationary PMV is a dead PMV

---

## Historical Note

The Bushmaster was developed by Thales Australia and has seen extensive service with the ADF in Afghanistan and Iraq. Its V-hull design has saved numerous lives from IED attacks.
