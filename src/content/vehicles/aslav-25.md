## Overview

The ASLAV-25 (Australian Light Armoured Vehicle) is an ADF-exclusive infantry fighting vehicle based on the LAV-25. It serves as the primary reconnaissance and infantry support vehicle for Australian forces in Squad.

**Key Feature**: The ASLAV-25 is **amphibious** and can traverse water obstacles.

## Weapons Systems

### M242 Bushmaster 25mm Autocannon

The main weapon is highly effective against infantry and light vehicles:
- **AP (Armor Piercing)**: 70 rounds - Can penetrate IFVs and APCs
- **HE (High Explosive)**: 230 rounds - Devastating against infantry

### FN MAG 7.62mm Coaxial
- 800 rounds available
- Good for engaging infantry when conserving 25mm

---

## Armor & Protection

The ASLAV is a "glass cannon" - powerful firepower but light armor:
- **All-round protection** against small arms fire
- **Vulnerable** to heavy machine guns, autocannons, and all AT weapons
- **Front** offers slightly better protection

---

## Amphibious Operations

The ASLAV-25 can swim across rivers and water obstacles:
- Enter water slowly at a shallow angle
- Maintain forward momentum in water
- Exit carefully - don't get stuck on banks
- Avoid deep water with strong currents

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use mobility** - don't stand still in firefights
2. **Flank enemies** rather than engaging head-on
3. **Support infantry** by suppressing enemy positions
4. **Use smoke** to break contact when damaged
5. **Exploit amphibious capability** for unexpected flanking routes
6. **Hunter-Killer system** allows commander to designate targets for gunner
