## Overview

The **ASLAV-25** (Australian Light Armoured Vehicle) is an 8×8 wheeled IFV exclusive to the Australian Defence Force. Based on the LAV-25, it's the fastest wheeled IFV in Squad at **100 km/h**.

**Key Features:**
- **Amphibious** - Can swim without preparation
- **Hunter-Killer** - Commander's stabilized periscope can designate targets
- **11 total capacity** (4 crew + 7 passengers)
- **600 ammo points** for resupply

## Hunter-Killer System

The ASLAV-25 features the **Hunter-Killer** capability:
1. Commander spots a target through stabilized periscope
2. Commander designates target
3. Gunner's turret automatically slews to target
4. Gunner engages

This allows rapid target acquisition and engagement.

---

## Amphibious Capability

The ASLAV-25 is one of only two amphibious LAV variants in Squad (with LAV-25):
- Enter water at shallow angles
- Maintain forward momentum
- Exit carefully on banks
- Enables water-based flanking routes

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use 100 km/h speed** for rapid repositioning
2. **Hunter-Killer** for fast target engagement
3. **Flank rather than charge** - light armor
4. **Amphibious flanks** catch enemies off guard
5. **Commander spots** while gunner engages
6. **Similar to LAV 6.0** but without pintle MG
