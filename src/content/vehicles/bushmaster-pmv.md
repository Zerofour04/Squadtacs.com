## Overview

The **Bushmaster PMV** (Protected Mobility Vehicle) is an Australian 4×4 light attack vehicle. Introduced in V2.15 for ADF, it comes in 3 variants with Mag58 or M2 RWS armament.

**Key Features:**

- **3 variants** - Mag58, Mag58 x3, M2 RWS
- **11 passengers** - Large transport capacity
- **V-shaped hull** - IED protection
- **No crewman kit** required
- **ADF exclusive**
- **Run-flat tyres** - Keeps moving with flats

## Gameplay

- **No crewman kit** required
- 750 HP - light armor protection
- Large passenger capacity (10-11)
- **M2 RWS** is stabilized with rangefinder
- Mag58 x3 = 360° coverage
- Good for rapid troop transport
- 300 ammo points for resupply

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **M2 RWS** - use stabilization advantage
2. **Mag58 x3** - man all positions for coverage
3. **Transport focus** - 11 passengers
4. **Light armor** - avoid heavy weapons
5. **Rangefinder** - M2 RWS only
6. **BDC marks** - left side of lines = impact
