## Overview

The **MT-LBM** is the modernized variant of the Soviet MT-LB. Developed by Muromteplovoz in the 1990s, it features improved turrets with KPVT HMG or 2A72 30mm autocannon. Added in A9.

**Key Features:**

- **3 variants** - 6MA, 6MA S8, 6MB
- **Modernized turret** - Better firepower than MT-LB
- **6MB has 30mm** - Shreds light armor
- **Amphibious** - Water-capable
- **Weak armor** - 14mm, lighter than BTR-80
- **Multi-faction** - RGF, IMF, GFI

## Gameplay

- **6MA** - KPVT handles infantry and light vehicles
- **6MA S8** - Rockets for burst damage
- **6MB** - 30mm shreds light armor
- **Weak armor** - Avoid IFV fights
- **Slower than BTR** - Plan approaches
- **Very loud** - Enemy hears early
- **Crewman required** - All variants
- **Community name** - "Track" or "Shitbox"

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **6MB vs light armor** - 30mm very effective
2. **Avoid M2A3/FV510** - Can't pen frontal armor
3. **Use infantry support** - Cover weaknesses
4. **Don't brawl** - Weaker than BTR-82A
5. **Flanking attacks** - 30mm shreds sides
6. **6MA S8 rockets** - Burst then retreat
7. **Smoke launchers** - Disengage when damaged
8. **Support vehicle** - Not main assault
