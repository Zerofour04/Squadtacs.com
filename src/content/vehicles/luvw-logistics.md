# LUVW Logistics

The **LUVW Logistics** is a light logistics vehicle used by the Canadian Armed Forces in Squad. Based on the Mercedes G-Wagon, it provides a faster but smaller capacity alternative to the MSVS.

## Tactical Notes

- Faster than MSVS but carries fewer supplies
- Good for quick FOB resupply runs
- Better off-road capability than larger trucks
- Unarmed - requires escort in contested areas
