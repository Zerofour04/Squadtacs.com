## Overview

The **Cobra-II** is a Turkish 4×4 light attack vehicle manufactured by Otokar. Introduced in V7.0 for Turkish Land Forces, it comes in 4 variants with M2 or MG3 armament in RWS or open-top configurations.

**Key Features:**

- **4 variants** - M2 RWS, M2, MG3 RWS, MG3
- **RWS options** - Protected gunner position
- **Open-top options** - Higher ammo capacity
- **10 passengers** - Large transport capacity
- **No crewman kit** required
- **TLF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **No crew required** - Anyone can operate
- **Smoke is driver-controlled** - Not gunner
- **RWS variants** - Gunner protected but less ammo
- **Open-top variants** - More ammo but exposed
- 750 HP - Light armor only
- 10 passengers for transport
- Similar role to M-ATV, TAPV

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **M2 variants** - Better vs light vehicles
2. **MG3 variants** - Better sustained fire vs infantry
3. **RWS** - Protected gunner, use at range
4. **Open-top** - More ammo, avoid direct fire
5. **Driver smokes** - Cover dismount/retreat
6. **10 passengers** - Good troop transport
7. **Light armor** - Avoid heavy weapons
