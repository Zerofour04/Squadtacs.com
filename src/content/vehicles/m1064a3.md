## Overview

The **M1064A3** is a self-propelled mortar carrier based on the M113 chassis. Introduced in Version 8.0 for US Army and Turkish Land Forces, it provides mobile indirect fire support with a 120mm mortar system.

**Key Features:**

- **120mm mortar** - M121 heavy mortar system
- **M2A1 .50 cal** - Open-top armored turret for self-defense
- **3 ammo types** - HE, HE near-surface, Smoke
- **2000m range** - Maximum range on flat ground
- **12 rounds/min** - High fire rate
- **US Army + TLF**

## Fire Control

The M1064A3 features an advanced mortar fire control system:

- **Deviation indicator** - Shows potential spread from target center
- **Time-to-impact** - Active countdown after firing
- **Flight time display** - Shows travel time before firing
- **Elevation/azimuth** - Precise aiming controls

## Gameplay

- **Mobile artillery** - Repositionable mortar platform
- **High ammo cost** - 50 ammo per round (all types)
- **Requires SL marks** - Fire on Squad Leader markers
- **Indirect fire** - Cannot see targets directly
- **Vulnerable** - Light M113 armor, avoid frontlines
- **Communication critical** - Coordinate with infantry

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Stay behind lines** - M113 armor won't survive combat
2. **Use terrain** - Hide behind hills, buildings
3. **Rearm frequently** - 50 ammo per round depletes fast
4. **HE for infantry** - Standard choice for enemies
5. **Near-surface** - Better against dug-in positions
6. **Smoke for cover** - Support advancing infantry
7. **.50 cal defense** - Last resort, relocate if engaged
8. **Mark targets** - Work with SLs for accurate fire
