## Overview

The M1128 Mobile Gun System (MGS) is a Stryker variant equipped with a 105mm cannon in an unmanned turret. It provides direct fire support capability with the mobility advantage of the wheeled Stryker platform, filling the gap between infantry and main battle tanks.

## Weapons Systems

### M68A2 105mm Rifled Cannon

The main armament fires two types of ammunition:

- **AP (Armor Piercing)**: 18 rounds - For engaging armored vehicles
- **HEAT (High Explosive Anti-Tank)**: 18 rounds - Effective against vehicles and fortifications

### M240C Coaxial Machine Gun

- 7.62mm belt-fed machine gun
- 400 rounds available
- Effective against infantry

### M2A1 Commander Weapon Station

- .50 caliber heavy machine gun
- 200 rounds available
- Independent operation by commander

## Armor & Protection

The M1128 shares the Stryker's armor profile:

- **Frontal Arc**: Protection against heavy machine guns
- **Side/Rear**: Vulnerable to RPGs and autocannons
- **Note**: Lighter armor than tracked vehicles - use mobility

### Smoke Systems

- Smoke grenade launchers for concealment

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Stay at range** - 105mm is effective at distance, close combat is risky
2. **Use terrain** - Hull-down positions maximize effectiveness
3. **Avoid MBTs** - Your armor won't withstand main gun hits
4. **Support infantry** - HEAT rounds devastate enemy positions
5. **Hit and run** - Use Stryker mobility to reposition after firing
6. **Flank enemy armor** - Side shots are more effective
7. **Coordinate with scouts** - Let others spot targets for you
