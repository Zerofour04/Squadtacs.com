## Overview

The **M1128 MGS** (Mobile Gun System) is a US Army fire support vehicle. Introduced in Version 7.2, it mounts a 105mm cannon on the Stryker chassis. Provides direct fire support with wheeled mobility.

**Key Features:**

- **105mm M68A2** - Same gun as M60 Patton tank
- **4-man crew** - Driver, gunner, loader, commander
- **Only 2 need crewman kit** - Driver and gunner
- **M2A1 loader MG** - .50 cal for self-defense
- **800mm penetration** - APFSDS matches MBT damage
- **US Army exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **Fire support** - Not a frontline tank
- **105mm power** - APFSDS has MBT-level penetration
- **Loader MG** - .50 cal for infantry defense
- **Wheeled mobility** - Fast repositioning
- **Light armor** - Avoid IFV autocannons
- **2 crewman only** - Loader/commander any kit

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Stay at range** - 105mm effective at distance
2. **Hull-down** - Expose only turret
3. **Avoid MBTs** - Can't survive tank rounds
4. **HEAT for bunkers** - 10 rounds vs fortifications
5. **APFSDS sparingly** - Only 6 rounds
6. **Loader MG** - Suppression/infantry defense
7. **Hit and run** - Reposition after engaging
8. **Flank armor** - Side shots more effective
