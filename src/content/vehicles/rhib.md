## Overview

The RHIB (Rigid-Hulled Inflatable Boat) is a fast watercraft used for transport, patrol, and logistics across water obstacles. Multiple variants exist with different weapon configurations.

---

## Variants (ADF)

### RHIB Transport
- **Weapon**: None
- **Passengers**: 6
- **Role**: Basic water transport

### RHIB M240
- **Weapon**: M240 7.62mm
- **Passengers**: 4
- **Role**: Armed patrol boat

### RHIB M2A1
- **Weapon**: M2A1 .50 Cal
- **Passengers**: 4
- **Role**: Heavy armed patrol

### RHIB Logistics
- **Weapon**: None
- **Capacity**: 600 supplies
- **Role**: Water-based logistics

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **High speed** makes RHIBs hard to hit
2. **Use waterways** for flanking maneuvers
3. **Don't stop in open water** - you're exposed
4. **Beach carefully** - getting stuck is embarrassing
5. **Logistics RHIB** can build riverside FOBs
6. **Cover amphibious landings** with armed variants

---

## Common to Many Factions

The RHIB is used by most conventional factions in Squad.
