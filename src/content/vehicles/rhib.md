## Overview

The **RHIB** (Rigid Hull Inflatable Boat) is a fast watercraft for amphibious operations. Introduced in V3.0 (Amphibious Assault), it comes in 10 variants with different weapon systems across multiple factions.

**Key Features:**

- **10 variants** - Transport, Logistics, 8 armed versions
- **70+ km/h** - Very fast on water
- **1 ticket** - All variants
- **90° firing arc** - Armed variants can only fire forward (45° left/right)
- **Multi-faction** - Most conventional factions

## Gameplay

- **90° firing arc** - Guns only aim forward (45° each side)
- **Vulnerable** - No armor, small arms damage
- **Very fast** - 70+ km/h makes accurate shots difficult
- **Beach landings** - Suppress infantry on approach
- **Ambush potential** - Speed + HMG from unexpected angles
- **All 1 ticket** - Very expendable

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Forward-only firing** - Point bow at targets
2. **High speed** - Hard to hit while moving
3. **Beach assaults** - Suppress landing zones
4. **Flank waterways** - Use rivers/coast for surprise
5. **Don't stop** - Sitting target on open water
6. **Logistics RHIB** - Build riverside FOBs
7. **M2/NSV/QJZ89** - 12.7mm for light vehicles
8. **M240B/PKP** - More ammo for sustained fire
