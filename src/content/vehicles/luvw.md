# LUVW

The **LUVW** (Light Utility Vehicle Wheeled) is a light utility vehicle used by the Canadian Armed Forces in Squad. Based on the Mercedes-Benz G-Class, it serves multiple roles from transport to light combat.

## Variants

### LUVW Transport
- No weapons
- 4 passengers + driver
- 300 ammo points

### LUVW Logistics
- No weapons
- Enlarged roof rack for supplies
- Supply transport capability

### LUVW M2 Open Top
- M2 Browning .50 cal with gun shield
- Exposed gunner position

### LUVW C6 Open Top
- C6 GPMG 7.62mm with gun shield
- Exposed gunner position

---

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- One of the fastest vehicles in game
- Light armor provides some protection against small arms
- Low profile compared to larger vehicles
- Quick respawn time

**Weaknesses:**
- Windows not bulletproof (5.56mm can penetrate)
- Open-top variants expose gunner
- Limited armor protection
- Cannot carry many passengers

---

## Tactical Notes

- The LUVW replaced the Volkswagen Iltis in Canadian service (2003)
- Based on Mercedes-Benz G-Class (G-Wagon)
- Use speed to avoid engagement rather than armor
- Armed variants have gun shields but gunner is still vulnerable
- Good for rapid deployment and reconnaissance
- Transport/Logistics variants have 300 ammo points
