## Overview

The **LUVW** (Light Utility Vehicle Wheeled), also called **G-Wagen**, is the Canadian light multi-purpose vehicle. Introduced in V2.16 for the Canadian Army, it is an up-armored Mercedes-Benz G-Class replacing the Volkswagen Iltis.

**Key Features:**

- **4 variants** - Transport, Logistics, M2, C6
- **No crewman kit** required
- **Fastest vehicle** - One of fastest in game
- **Light armor** - Better than trucks, worse than M-ATV
- **Gun shield** - Armed variants have protection
- **CAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **No crewman kit** - Any kit can drive/gun
- **Fastest vehicle** - One of fastest in game
- **Light armor** - Better than logi trucks
- **Windows vulnerable** - 5.56mm guaranteed penetration
- **Gun shield** - Armed variants have some protection
- **Open-top** - Gunner still exposed
- **Logi variant cheap** - Only 1 ticket

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use speed** - Fastest vehicle, avoid combat
2. **Windows not safe** - 5.56mm penetrates
3. **M2 variant** - Anti-vehicle capability
4. **C6 variant** - Anti-infantry
5. **Logi cheap** - Only 1 ticket, small runs
6. **Gun shield** - Better than nothing
7. **No crewman** - Anyone can operate
8. **G-Wagen base** - Mercedes-Benz G-Class
