# CH-146 Griffon

The **CH-146 Griffon** is a utility helicopter used by the Canadian Armed Forces in Squad. Based on the Bell 412, it features a reconnaissance camera system and C6 door guns.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Very agile and maneuverable
- 360° reconnaissance camera for co-pilot
- Floaty handling makes precision flying easier
- Higher pitch/roll/yaw rates than larger helicopters

**Weaknesses:**
- Lowest supply capacity (800 points)
- Smaller than other transport helicopters
- Limited passenger capacity compared to Mi-8/UH-60

---

## Tactical Notes

- The CH-146 has the most UH-1 like flight model in CAF service
- Use the reconnaissance camera to scout enemy positions
- Camera can rotate 360° - excellent for relaying tank positions
- Best suited for quick FOB establishment, then reconnaissance
- Door gunners have C6 MGs with 8x 100-round boxes each
- Based on Bell 412 helicopter, in service since 1992
- 260 km/h (160 mph) top speed, 17m length
