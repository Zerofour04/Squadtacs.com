## Overview

The **CH-146 Griffon** is a Canadian medium-lift helicopter based on the Bell 412. Added in Alpha V15.3 as part of the Canadian Army DLC, it comes in 3 variants with different armament options.

**Key Features:**

- **3 variants** - Transport, CAS, Raven
- **Scout camera** - 360° rotation for co-pilot
- **800 total supplies** - Least of any helicopter (400/400)
- **Most maneuverable** - Highest pitch/yaw/roll rate
- **"Floaty" handling** - Weight to power ratio
- **CAF and WPMC** - Dual faction availability

## Flight Characteristics

- **Similar to UH-1** - Base flight model
- **Higher maneuverability** - Increased pitch, yaw, roll vs UH-60M
- **"Floaty" handling** - Weight to power ratio
- **Most maneuverable** aircraft in Squad
- **260 km/h** top speed (160 mph)

---

## Gameplay

- **Scout camera** - 360° co-pilot reconnaissance
- **800 supply capacity** - Least of any helicopter
- Suited for quick initial FOBs
- Excellent for aerial reconnaissance
- Co-pilot spots for ground armor squads
- **Raven** - Pilot exposed (no doors)
- CAS variant = heavy firepower

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Scout camera** - Use co-pilot for reconnaissance
2. **Quick FOBs** - Limited supplies, fast insertion
3. **Spot enemy armor** - Help ground forces
4. **Raven caution** - Keep nose toward threats
5. **Maneuverability** - Use agility to evade
6. **CAS firepower** - GAU-21 devastates light vehicles
