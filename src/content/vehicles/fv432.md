# FV432 Bulldog

The FV432 Bulldog is a tracked armored personnel carrier that has served the British Army since the 1960s. It provides good protection and carries a full infantry section.

## Specifications

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Tickets | 6 |
| Crew | 2 (Driver, Gunner) |
| Passengers | 10 |
| HP | 1250 |
| Respawn | 6 min |

## Variants

### FV432 (Standard)
- **Armament**: L7A2 7.62mm GPMG (open top)

### FV432 RWS
- **Armament**: M2A1 .50 cal in Remote Weapon Station

## Features

- Tracked for good off-road mobility
- Heavy armor for an APC
- Large passenger capacity
- Slow but reliable

## Tactical Notes

The Bulldog is excellent for transporting infantry through dangerous areas. The RWS variant provides better gunner protection and heavier firepower.
