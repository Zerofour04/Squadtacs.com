## Overview

The **FV432 Bulldog** is a British tracked armored personnel carrier from the FV430 series. Introduced in 1963 and still in service, it provides infantry transport with light fire support capability.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - .50cal open-top, L37A2 RWS
- **11 passengers** - Large transport capacity
- **No crewman kit** - .50cal variant requires no kit
- **Driver smoke** - Smoke generator (LMB)
- **Surprisingly fast** - Good off-road mobility
- **BAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **No crewman kit** - .50cal variant
- **1 crewman** - RWS variant (for RWS operation)
- **Driver smoke** - Hold LMB when engine on
- **Fast for APC** - Good tracked mobility
- **.50cal exposed** - Well-aimed shot kills gunner
- **RWS protected** - Gunner safe from fire
- **2000 HP** - Decent armor protection

## Tactical Tips

1. **.50cal** - Engage light vehicles, avoid IFVs
2. **RWS** - Safer but less firepower
3. **Smoke escape** - Driver controls smoke generator
4. **Flank BTRs** - Don't engage head-on
5. **Protect gunner** - .50cal is exposed
6. **11 passengers** - Great troop transport
7. **Use mobility** - Faster than wheeled on rough terrain
