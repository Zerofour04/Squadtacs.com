## Overview

The **Safir** is a fictional light attack vehicle created for Squad, similar to the Iranian Fath Safir. Introduced in V1.0, it serves as the GFI's light utility and attack vehicle with 4 variants.

**Key Features:**

- **4 variants** - Logistics, MG3, Kord, Kornet
- **Fictional vehicle** - Created for Squad
- **Light armor** - Damaged by small arms
- **Least protection** - Similar to LUV-A1 and Minsk 400
- **Fast and maneuverable** - Fits in urban areas
- **GFI exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **Light vehicle** - Damaged by small arms
- **Least protection** - No armor, exposed occupants
- **Very fast** - Good for flanking
- **Urban capable** - Fits in alleys and sheds
- **Front-only firing** - MG variants restricted arc
- **Kornet** - Can kill any tank
- **No crewman required** - Any kit can drive

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Don't engage head-on** - No protection
2. **Flank and retreat** - Hit and run
3. **Urban advantage** - Fits in tight spaces
4. **Kord vs light armor** - 12.7mm effective
5. **Kornet ambush** - Position behind cover
6. **Logistics runs** - Fast small supply
7. **MG suppression** - Gunner shield helps
8. **Front-only arc** - Position vehicle correctly
