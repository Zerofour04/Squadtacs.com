## Overview

The **BTR-82A** is a modern Russian 8×8 wheeled IFV. Added in Alpha V9, it's a modernized BTR-80 with 30mm autocannon. Sacrifices armor for speed - faster but weaker than dedicated IFVs like M2A3 or FV510.

**Key Features:**

- **30mm 2A72 autocannon** - 300 rounds (150 AP + 150 HE)
- **102 km/h top speed** - Fastest wheeled IFV
- **8×8 wheeled** - Excellent road mobility
- **Engine smoke generator** - Driver-controlled
- **RGF exclusive** - Only Russian faction
- **Light armor** - 1000 HP (vs 2000 HP M2A3)

## Gameplay

- **Fast troop transport** with autocannon
- Not a dedicated IFV - struggles vs M2A3/FV510
- 1000 HP = half the health of Western IFVs
- **Retreat immediately** if taking heavy fire
- 30mm does less damage than Western 25mm
- Engine smoke covers retreat or allies
- Commonly called "30 mil BTR" or "30 mike"

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Speed is your armor** - don't trade shots
2. **Flank and disengage** - retreat from IFVs
3. **Engine smoke** - cover retreat or allies
4. **Turret weak point** - ~20 shots to disable M2A3
5. **FV510 left cheek** - weak armor spot
6. **Commander calls targets** - let gunner focus
7. **Transport infantry** - you're an APC with teeth
