## Overview

The **Technical** is an improvised fighting vehicle based on civilian pickup trucks (Toyota Hilux). Used by INS, IMF, and WPMC for transportation, logistics, and fire support with many weapon variants.

**Key Features:**

- **Multiple variants** - Transport, logistics, and 5+ armed versions
- **Fastest vehicle** - Superior speed and maneuverability
- **No armor** - Vulnerable to all weapons
- **No crewman kit** - Any kit can drive/gun
- **Armored variants** - Since V11
- **INS/IMF/WPMC**

## Gameplay

- **Fastest in game** - Superior speed and maneuverability
- **No armor** - Any weapon can damage/destroy
- **Armored variants** - Protection from small arms (V11+)
- **Exposed gunners** - All weapon variants
- **Hit-and-run** - SPG-9 and DShK tactics
- **Braking spins** - Release throttle instead

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use speed** - Don't stop in the open
2. **Hit-and-run** - Fire and relocate
3. **Ambush** - SPG-9 from concealment
4. **Hunt logistics** - Behind enemy lines
5. **Armored windows** - Can be shot through by .50 cal
6. **Don't brake hard** - Causes spin-out
7. **UB-32 spotter** - Use with SL binoculars
8. **ZU-23-2** - Effective vs all but MBTs
