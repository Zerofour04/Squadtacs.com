## Overview

The **M113A3** is a tracked APC used by multiple factions. A Cold War-era design still in service, it provides cheap tracked transport. The Turkish variant is designated **M113A2T** but functions identically.

**Key Features:**

- **5 variants** - TLAV, M2, Mk19, C6, Logistics
- **Tracked** - Better off-road than wheeled APCs
- **Light armor** - Very thin (10-15mm)
- **5 tickets** - Cheap transport
- **Multi-faction** - CAF, USA, TLF, WPMC

## Gameplay

- **Tracked mobility** - Better on rough terrain
- **Light armor** - Similar to MT-LB family
- **Cheap ticket cost** - Only 5 tickets
- **TLAV best variant** - RWS protected
- **Open-top exposed** - M2, Mk19, C6 gunners vulnerable
- **Logistics armored** - Better than trucks
- **Smoke generator** - Driver can deploy smoke

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **TLAV preferred** - Only protected gunner
2. **Avoid HMGs** - Thin armor penetrated
3. **Tracked advantage** - Off-road mobility
4. **Open-top caution** - Gunners easily killed
5. **Use for transport** - Not for combat
6. **Logi variant** - Armored supply runs
7. **Similar to MT-LB** - Same role/protection
8. **Smoke to retreat** - Driver smoke generator
