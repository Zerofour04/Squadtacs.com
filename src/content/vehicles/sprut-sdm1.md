## Overview

The **Sprut-SDM1** is a Russian airborne assault gun based on the BMD-4M chassis. Introduced in V5.0, it is the first Mobile Gun System class vehicle for VDV, featuring MBT-level firepower on a light, amphibious platform.

**Key Features:**

- **125mm 2A45 main gun** - Same firepower as MBTs
- **Gun-launched ATGM** - 9M119M Refleks for long range
- **Amphibious** - Fully water-capable
- **Stabilized commander optic** - Like BMD-4M
- **800mm penetration** - APFSDS same as tanks
- **VDV exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **Tank destroyer role** - MBT gun on light platform
- **10 tickets** - Cheaper than MBTs (15)
- **Amphibious** - Cross water obstacles
- **Light armor** - 1250 HP vulnerable to IFVs
- **ATGM range** - Outrange enemy armor
- **No passengers** - Pure combat vehicle
- **600 ammo capacity** - Higher than MBTs

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Engage from range** - Don't frontline
2. **Fire first** - ATGM outranges tanks
3. **Use flanking** - Amphibious routes
4. **Relocate often** - Light armor
5. **Frag vs infantry** - Conserve APFSDS
6. **Avoid MBT duels** - Can't survive return fire
7. **Hunter-Killer** - Commander spots, gunner engages
8. **Calculate risks** - 10 vs 15 tickets
