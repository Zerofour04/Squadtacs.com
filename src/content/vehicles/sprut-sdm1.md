# Sprut-SDM1

The **Sprut-SDM1** (2S25M) is an air-droppable self-propelled anti-tank gun exclusive to the VDV in Squad. Armed with the same 125mm cannon as the T-72, it provides airborne forces with MBT-level firepower while maintaining full amphibious capability.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Full 125mm tank gun on a light chassis - MBT firepower at IFV cost
- Gun-launched 9M119M ATGM for long-range armor kills
- Fully amphibious - can cross water obstacles
- Lower ticket cost than MBTs (10 vs 15)
- Higher ammo rearm capacity (600 pts) than MBTs
- Air-droppable design fits VDV doctrine

**Weaknesses:**
- Light armor (1250 HP) - vulnerable to IFV autocannons
- No commander weapon - limited anti-infantry capability
- Small ammunition loadout (34 main gun rounds vs ~40 on MBT)
- Cannot survive frontal engagement with enemy MBTs

---

## Tactical Notes

- Use the Sprut as a tank destroyer, not a frontline tank - engage from range and relocate
- The 9M119M ATGM allows you to outrange enemy armor - fire first, kill first
- Amphibious capability enables flanking routes unavailable to tracked MBTs
- At 10 tickets vs 15 for an MBT, the Sprut is more expendable - take calculated risks
- Avoid direct confrontation with enemy tanks - your armor cannot survive their return fire
- Use Frag rounds against infantry and light vehicles to conserve APFSDS
- Exclusive to VDV faction
