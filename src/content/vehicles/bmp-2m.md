## Overview

The **BMP-2M "Berezhok"** is a modernized BMP-2 with the B05Ya01 Berezhok turret. Introduced in V8.0 for RGF, it upgrades the standard BMP-2 with Kornet ATGMs, AGS-30 grenade launcher, and improved optics (same as BMD-4).

**Key Features:**

- **30mm 2A42 autocannon** - Same as BMP-2 (500 rounds)
- **9M133 Kornet ATGM** - 4 missiles, higher pen than Konkurs
- **AGS-30 grenade launcher** - 120 rounds for infantry
- **Improved optics** - Day/night sights from BMD-4
- **RGF exclusive** - Premium IFV upgrade

## Gameplay

- **Premium RGF IFV** - significant upgrade over BMP-2
- **Kornet defeats MBT fronts** (unlike Konkurs)
- **AGS-30** for area denial and infantry
- Same chassis/armor as BMP-2 (1250 HP)
- Don't overcommit - still light armor
- Use Kornet range advantage vs MBTs
- AGS-30 effective for FOB/HAB denial

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Engage MBTs from range** with Kornet
2. **Use AGS-30** to suppress infantry positions
3. **30mm AP** for IFV duels
4. **30mm HE** for infantry cleanup
5. **Commander seat** doesn't need Crewman kit
6. **Support role** - not a tank killer up close
7. **Same weaknesses** as BMP-2 (light armor)
