# BMD-4M

The **BMD-4M** is the primary infantry fighting vehicle of the VDV in Squad. It features a powerful Bakhcha-U turret combining a 30mm autocannon with a 100mm low-velocity gun capable of firing ATGMs, all on an amphibious, air-droppable chassis.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Bakhcha-U turret combines autocannon + ATGM in one package
- 9M117 Bastion ATGM can engage tanks at range
- 100mm HE shells devastate infantry and light vehicles
- 30mm autocannon effective against IFVs and helicopters
- Fully amphibious - crosses rivers and lakes
- 7 passenger capacity for infantry squad

**Weaknesses:**
- Light armor (1250 HP) - HMG fire can damage it
- Limited ATGM count (only 3 missiles)
- 100mm has low muzzle velocity - difficult to hit moving targets
- Cramped interior limits dismount protection

---

## Tactical Notes

- The BMD-4M is a multi-role platform - use 30mm against vehicles, 100mm against infantry, ATGM against armor
- Only 3 ATGMs means every shot must count - don't waste them on light vehicles
- Use amphibious capability to approach from unexpected angles
- The 30mm AP can penetrate most IFVs - engage them confidently at close-medium range
- 100mm HE has splash damage - aim near infantry, not directly at them
- Dismount infantry before engaging enemy armor - they cannot contribute and add to ticket loss if destroyed
- Exclusive to VDV faction
