## Overview

The **BMD-4M** is a modernized airborne IFV. Introduced in V5.0 for the VDV, it combines the Bakhcha-U dual-barrel weapon system (same as BMP-3) with an amphibious, air-droppable platform.

**Key Features:**

- **Amphibious** - Crosses water obstacles
- **3 crew + 7 passengers** - Full squad transport
- **Dual-barrel system** - 30mm autocannon + 100mm gun/ATGM
- **Commander stabilization** - Unlike BMD-1M
- **Can be one-shot by TOW** in rear (turret thrown off)

## Gameplay

- Commander optic **IS stabilized** (unlike BMD-1M)
- TOW to rear = instant kill (turret thrown)
- TOW to rear-hull top = cook-off only
- Use amphibious for flanking approaches
- 30mm defeats most IFVs at close-medium range
- 100mm low velocity - lead moving targets
- Dismount infantry before armor engagement
