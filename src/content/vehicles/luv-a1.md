## Overview

The **LUV-A1** (Light Utility Vehicle) is the Canadian light utility vehicle. Introduced in A15.3 for the Canadian Army, it is a militarized M-Gator used for transport and supply roles.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - Transport and Logistics
- **No armor** - Completely unprotected
- **Small & fast** - Quieter than trucks
- **Decent capacity** - Good supplies for size
- **Similar to technicals** - IMF/INS equivalent
- **CAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **No armor** - Zero protection
- **No weapons** - Cannot self-defend
- **Small size** - Less visible than trucks
- **Quiet** - Less noise than logi trucks
- **Fast** - Quick in/out of areas
- **Similar to technicals** - Like IMF/INS utility vehicles
- **Stay away from combat**

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **No protection** - Avoid all combat
2. **Transport variant** - Quick insertions
3. **Logi variant** - Small FOB runs
4. **Quiet & small** - Stealthier than trucks
5. **Similar to technicals** - Same utility role
6. **Don't expect survival** - Completely unarmored
7. **Use for forward FOBs** - Quick supply runs
