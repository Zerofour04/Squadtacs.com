## Overview

The **Quad Bike** is an ATV for fast transport and scouting. Introduced in V7.2, it serves USA, USMC, WPMC, and CRF. One of the fastest vehicles in Squad with minimal ticket cost.

**Key Features:**

- **2 seats** - Driver + 1 passenger
- **~80 km/h** - One of fastest vehicles
- **No SL approval** - Anyone can take
- **1 ticket** - Very expendable
- **150 ammo capacity** - Small resupply
- **Fast respawn** - Quick replacement
- **Multi-faction** - USA, USMC, WPMC, CRF

## Gameplay

- **No protection** - Driver exposed
- **Any kit** - No crewman required
- **No SL needed** - Take freely
- **1 ticket** - Minimal loss
- **Very fast** - ~80 km/h top speed
- **150 ammo** - Can do small resupply
- **Off-road** - 4 wheels more stable than motorbike

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Rally drops** - SL fast positioning
2. **Scout ahead** - Expendable recon
3. **Flanking** - Speed behind enemy lines
4. **Small resupply** - 150 ammo for FOB
5. **Abandon freely** - Only 1 ticket
6. **Use roads** - Faster on pavement
7. **Avoid combat** - Zero protection
8. **More stable** - Than Minsk 400 motorbike
