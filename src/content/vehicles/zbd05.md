# ZBD05

The **ZBD05** (Type 05) is a high-speed amphibious infantry fighting vehicle designed for the PLA Navy Marine Corps and PLA Amphibious Ground Force. Its unique planing hull design allows water speeds of 30 km/h - the fastest amphibious speed of any vehicle in Squad.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Fastest water speed in Squad (30 km/h)
- Planing hull design for high-speed amphibious assault
- HJ-73C ATGM capability
- Carries 7 passengers
- Commander doesn't require Crewman kit

**Weaknesses:**
- Light armor
- Optimized for amphibious ops, less capable on land than dedicated IFVs
- Large profile when bow planes are deployed

---

## Tactical Notes

- The ZBD05 is specifically designed for rapid amphibious assault - use its 30 km/h water speed to cross water obstacles faster than any other vehicle
- Ideal for amphibious layers on maps like Skorpo, Narva, and Pacific island maps
- Commander position does not require Crewman kit - useful for infantry squads conducting beach assaults
- Shares the same turret and armament as ZBD04A but in a dedicated amphibious hull
- Available to PLA, PLANMC, and PLAAGF factions
