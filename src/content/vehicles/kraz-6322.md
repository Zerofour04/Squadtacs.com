## Overview

The **KrAZ-6322** is a Ukrainian multi-purpose military truck. Introduced in Version 10.0 (Trident Strike) for Armed Forces of Ukraine, it serves as the backbone of AFU logistics and mobility, including as the chassis for the BM-21 Grad rocket artillery.

**Key Features:**

- **3 variants** - Transport, Logistics, BM-21 Grad
- **Multi-role** - Transport, supply, artillery
- **BM-21 chassis** - Rocket artillery platform
- **No armor** - Vulnerable to all weapons
- **No weapons** - Cannot self-defend
- **AFU exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **No armor** - Unarmored truck
- **No weapons** - Transport/Logi variants
- **Small arms vulnerable** - Engine/tires disabled by rifle fire
- **Stay behind lines** - No combat capability
- **Multi-role platform** - Transport, logi, artillery
- **BM-21 version** - Commander asset

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Avoid combat** - Zero protection
2. **Front grille weak** - Rifle fire disables engine
3. **Transport variant** - Mass troop movement
4. **Logi variant** - FOB supply runs
5. **BM-21 variant** - Stay far from front
6. **Protect with escorts** - No self-defense
7. **AFU backbone** - Essential logistics
