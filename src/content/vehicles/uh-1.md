## Overview

The **UH-1** is a utility helicopter family in Squad. The UH-1Y Venom serves the USMC (introduced V3.0 Amphibious Assault), while the UH-1H is the TLF variant.

**Key Features:**

- **Scout camera** - First heli in Squad with 360° co-pilot camera
- **Most maneuverable** - Superior pitch, yaw, and roll rates
- **900 supply points** - Reduced capacity (only CH-146 is lower)
- **Door gunners** - M240H (USMC) or MG3 (TLF)
- **"Floaty" handling** - Weight to power ratio

## Flight Characteristics

- **Most maneuverable** - Superior to UH-60M
- **CH-146 flight model** - Increased pitch, yaw, roll rates
- **"Floaty" handling** - Weight to power ratio
- **Quick FOBs** - Best for initial rapid deployments
- **Recon role** - Scout camera for team intel

---

## Gameplay

- **900 supply points** - Only 450 ammo + 450 construction
- **Lowest capacity** - Only CH-146 (800) carries less
- **Quick FOBs** - Rapid initial deployments
- **Recon support** - Scout camera for armor spotting
- **High maneuverability** - Aggressive flying possible
- **Door gunners** - 80° arc forward and backward

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Quick FOBs** - Best for initial deployments
2. **Scout camera** - Co-pilot spots for ground armor
3. **Maneuverability** - Most agile heli in Squad
4. **Low supply** - Not ideal for sustained logistics
5. **Door gun arcs** - Can shoot almost forward/backward
6. **Recon role** - Communicate enemy positions
