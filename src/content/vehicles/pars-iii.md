## Overview

The **PARS III** is a Turkish 8x8 wheeled armored vehicle by FNSS. Introduced in V7.0, it serves as TLF's primary IFV/APC. Features 4 RWS variants with the 25mm being the most powerful.

**Key Features:**

- **4 variants** - 25mm IFV, M2 RWS, MG3 RWS, Mk19 RWS
- **103 km/h** - Fastest IFV in game
- **8x8 wheeled** - Excellent mobility
- **25mm Bushmaster** - Same as M2A3 Bradley
- **Smoke launchers** - Driver AND gunner
- **TLF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **103 km/h** - Fastest IFV (fastest by far)
- **25mm Bushmaster** - Same firepower as M2A3
- **8x8 wheeled** - Road speed advantage
- **RWS variants** - Protected gunner
- **Double smoke** - Driver and gunner
- **Light armor** - Similar to Stryker
- **Multiple turret options** - Mission flexibility

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use speed** - 103 km/h for flanking
2. **25mm vs IFVs** - Competitive with M2A3
3. **RWS variants** - Protected battle taxi
4. **Mk19** - Infantry suppression
5. **Double smoke** - Escape or assault
6. **Don't frontline** - Light armor
7. **Use mobility** - 8x8 advantage
8. **Roads preferred** - Wheeled vehicle
