# BMD-1M

The **BMD-1M** is a modernized version of the legendary BMD-1, the world's first airborne infantry fighting vehicle. Used exclusively by the VDV in Squad, it combines a 73mm low-pressure gun with full amphibious capability on a lightweight, air-droppable chassis.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- 73mm HEAT rounds effective against light vehicles and fortifications
- OG-15V frag rounds devastate infantry in the open
- Fully amphibious - crosses water obstacles
- 7 passenger capacity for full squad transport
- Lightweight and highly mobile

**Weaknesses:**
- No stabilization on commander optic (unlike BMD-4M)
- 73mm has very low muzzle velocity - difficult to hit moving targets
- Light armor (1250 HP) - vulnerable to autocannon fire
- No ATGM capability - cannot engage tanks at range
- Survives TOW hits unlike BMD-4M, but rear/top hits cause ammo cook-off

---

## Tactical Notes

- The 2A28 gun is the same as on the BMP-1 - learn the ballistics for accurate fire
- HEAT rounds are effective against APCs and IFVs at close range
- Use Frag rounds against infantry - the splash damage is significant
- Amphibious capability allows flanking routes across rivers and lakes
- Stay mobile - your thin armor won't protect you from sustained fire
- Commander's optic is unstabilized - stop the vehicle for accurate observation
- Exclusive to VDV faction
