## Overview

The **BMD-1M** is a modernized airborne IFV. Introduced in V5.0 for the VDV, it uses the same 2A28 73mm gun as the BMP-1 on a lightweight amphibious platform.

**Key Features:**

- **Amphibious** - Crosses water obstacles
- **2 crew + 7 passengers** - Full squad transport
- **73mm gun** - HEAT + Frag rounds
- **Cannot be one-shot by TOW** (unlike BMD-4M)

## Gameplay

- Commander optic is **NOT stabilized** (unlike BMD-4M)
- 73mm has low muzzle velocity - lead moving targets
- HEAT effective vs light armor
- Frag rounds devastate infantry
- Rear/top hits can cause ammo cook-off
