## Overview

The **Lynx 8×8** (CS/VP17) is the PLA amphibious all-terrain vehicle. Introduced in Version 10.3, it provides light transport and logistics capability for all three PLA factions.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - Transport and Logistics
- **Amphibious** - Can swim
- **Unarmed** - No weapons
- **8x8 ATV** - All-terrain mobility
- **Rollover danger** - Passengers dismount if flipped
- **PLA/PLAAGF/PLANMC**

## Amphibious Operation

- **Can swim** - 8x8 amphibious design
- **Entry caution** - High speed water entry can capsize
- **Rollover risk** - Players on outside dismounted
- **Stable entry** - Slow approach to water

---

## Gameplay

- **Unarmed** - No weapons, no self-defense
- **Amphibious** - Can cross water
- **Light armor** - Minimal protection
- **Rollover danger** - Outside passengers dismount
- **Similar to CPV** - Same rollover mechanics
- **All-terrain** - 8x8 mobility
- **PLA exclusive**

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Amphibious** - Use water crossings for flanks
2. **Slow water entry** - Prevent capsize
3. **Rollover caution** - Passengers fall off
4. **No weapons** - Avoid all combat
5. **Transport variant** - Fast insertions
6. **Logi variant** - Forward FOB supply
7. **Similar to CPV** - Same handling concerns
8. **Light armor** - Minimal protection
