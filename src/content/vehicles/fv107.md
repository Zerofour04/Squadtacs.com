## Overview

The **FV107 Scimitar** is a tracked reconnaissance vehicle from the Combat Vehicle Reconnaissance Tracked (CVRT) family. Added in V2.0 for British Armed Forces, it features a 30mm RARDEN cannon on a lightweight, highly mobile platform.

**Key Features:**

- **30mm RARDEN** - Same cannon as Warrior IFV
- **88 km/h top speed** - Faster than most MBTs
- **Tracked recon** - Excellent off-road capability
- **3 positions** - Driver, gunner, commander
- **Driver smoke** - Smoke generator (hold LMB)
- **BAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **No stabilization** - RARDEN cannot fire accurately on the move
- **No hunter-killer** - Commander has no override
- **Driver smoke** - Hold LMB to trail smoke
- **Very fast** - Can outrun most MBTs
- **Light armor** - Avoid heavy vehicles head-on
- **Ambush role** - Engage from concealment
- 750 HP = vulnerable to autocannons

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use speed** - Get to positions before enemies
2. **Ambush tactics** - Engage from concealment
3. **Target weak armor** - Rear/sides of heavy vehicles
4. **Support infantry** - Fire support from flanks
5. **Avoid direct fights** - Not an IFV or tank
6. **Smoke retreat** - Driver smoke for escape
7. **Recon first** - Spot for team, then engage
