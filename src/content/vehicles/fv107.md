# FV107 Scimitar

The FV107 Scimitar is a light reconnaissance vehicle from the CVR(T) family, armed with the same 30mm RARDEN cannon as the Warrior but on a much lighter, faster platform.

## Specifications

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Tickets | 6 |
| Crew | 3 (Driver, Gunner, Commander) |
| HP | 1000 |
| Respawn | 8 min |

## Armament

- **Main Gun**: 30mm RARDEN Cannon (AP + HE)
- **Coaxial**: L37A2 7.62mm

## Features

- Very fast and maneuverable
- Same gun as Warrior on lighter platform
- Light aluminum armor
- Excellent reconnaissance vehicle

## Tactical Notes

The Scimitar excels at flanking and reconnaissance. Its light armor means it should avoid direct confrontation with IFVs and MBTs. Use speed and positioning to your advantage.
