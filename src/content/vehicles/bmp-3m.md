## Overview

The **BMP-3M** is an upgraded BMP-3 with the Bakhcha-U turret. Introduced in V8.0 for RGF, it features the same dual-barrel weapon system as the BMD-4M: 30mm autocannon + 100mm gun/ATGM. Most heavily armed IFV in Squad.

**Key Features:**

- **30mm 2A72 autocannon** - 500 rounds (195 AP + 305 HE)
- **100mm 2A70 gun** - 22 HE + 3 Bastion ATGM
- **Hull PKT guns** - 2x forward-facing MGs
- **Stabilized commander optic** - Hunter-killer capability
- **ATGM autoloader** - Faster missile reload
- **Laser rangefinder** - Precise engagement

## Gameplay

- **Most firepower** of any Russian IFV
- Same turret as BMD-4M but on heavier chassis
- **Laser rangefinder** for accurate shots
- **Commander stabilization** - effective hunter-killer
- 100mm HE devastates infantry and light vehicles
- Bastion ATGM can engage armor
- Slower turret (35°/s) than other IFVs
- Still 1250 HP - avoid MBT frontal engagement

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use 30mm AP** vs IFVs and light armor
2. **Use 100mm HE** vs infantry clusters
3. **Reserve Bastion ATGM** for enemy armor
4. **Laser rangefinder** enables first-round hits
5. **Hull PKTs** provide additional forward firepower
6. **Commander** can designate targets for gunner
7. **Slower turret** - pre-aim before engagement
