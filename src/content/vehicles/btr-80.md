## Overview

The **BTR-80** is a Soviet 8×8 wheeled armored personnel carrier. Adopted by the Soviet Army in 1986, it remained standard APC until 2013 when replaced by the BTR-82A. Still widely used by Russian and irregular forces in Squad.

**Key Features:**

- **14.5mm KPVT** - Same firepower as BRDM-2
- **93 km/h top speed** - Fast road mobility
- **8×8 wheeled** - Good all-terrain capability
- **11 capacity** - 3 crew + 7 passengers
- **600 ammo points** - Resupply capability
- **Low ticket cost** - Only 5 tickets

## Gameplay

- **Armored Personnel Carrier** - transport focus
- Same KPVT as BRDM-2 but better armor
- 1000 HP - survives more punishment
- Cannot penetrate IFV frontal armor
- Effective vs infantry and light vehicles
- Loses 1v1 to 30mm autocannon APCs
- Transport friendlies to objectives
- 600 ammo points for infantry resupply

## Tactical Tips

1. **Transport role** - offer transport to infantry
2. **Fire in bursts** - 50-round belt is small
3. **Ambush tactics** - don't face IFVs frontally
4. **Target weak points** - roof, rear, lower hull
5. **Commander comms** - coordinate target calls
6. **Smoke defense** - 2 charges, don't panic-fire both
7. **600 ammo points** - resupply friendlies
