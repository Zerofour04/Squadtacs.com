## Overview

The **CSK131 Mengshi** is the second-generation Chinese infantry mobility vehicle developed by Dongfeng Motor Corporation. Introduced in V4.0 (Red Star Rising) for PLA factions, it evolved from a Humvee copy to an indigenous design with improved armor.

**Key Features:**

- **4 variants** - QJY88, QJZ89, QJC88 RWS, HJ-8
- **Multiple factions** - PLA, PLANMC, PLAAGF
- **HJ-8 ATGM** - 6 missiles for anti-armor
- **RWS option** - Protected gunner
- **No crewman kit** required
- **Beidou navigation** - Chinese GPS system
