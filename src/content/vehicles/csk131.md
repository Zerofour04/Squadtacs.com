# CSK131 Mengshi

The **CSK131** is the second-generation Mengshi infantry mobility vehicle developed by Dongfeng Motor Corporation for the PLA. Originally based on imported Humvee/Hummer H1 components, the second generation is a domestic design with improved armor protection.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Multiple weapon configurations
- Fast road speed
- HJ-8 variant has 6 ATGMs for anti-armor
- Low ticket cost
- No crew requirement

**Weaknesses:**
- Light armor (750 HP)
- Exposed gunner positions on most variants
- Vulnerable to all weapons

---

## Tactical Notes

- The CSK131 is comparable to the American Humvee/M1151
- The HJ-8 ATGM variant provides significant anti-armor capability with 6 guided missiles
- The RWS variant (QJC88) provides the gunner with protection via remote weapon station
- Central tire pressure system and Beidou satellite navigation equipped
- Night vision cameras installed for driver and rear door
