## Overview

The **Ural-375D** is a Soviet 6x6 off-road truck adopted by the Soviet Army in 1964. In Squad, it serves the IMF and INS as transport, logistics, and (for INS) anti-air platform.

**Key Features:**

- **Three variants** - Transport, Logistics, ZU-23-2
- **75 km/h** - Off-road capable
- **No armor** - Vulnerable to .50 cal and RPG
- **Crew exposed** - No protection
- **IMF/INS only** - Replaced Ural-4320 for RGF

## Gameplay

- **No armor** - Crew has no protection
- **Vulnerable** - .50 cal and RPG-7 can destroy
- **Crew exposed** - Small arms can kill driver/gunner
- **ZU-23-2** - Effective vs aircraft and infantry
- **Former RGF** - Replaced by Ural-4320

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **No protection** - Avoid combat zones
2. **Quick logi runs** - Drop supplies fast
3. **ZU-23-2** - Stay hidden, ambush aircraft
4. **Civilian colors** - INS logi may blend in
5. **Infantry escort** - Vehicle cannot defend itself
6. **Avoid .50 cal** - Will destroy quickly

---

## History

The Ural-375D replaced the ZIL-157 truck in 1964. Upgrades included all-metal cab, full-time AWD, and served as mount for BM-21 Grad rocket launcher. Production stopped 1983 when Ural-4320 took over. Originally used by RGF in Squad before being replaced by Ural-4320.
