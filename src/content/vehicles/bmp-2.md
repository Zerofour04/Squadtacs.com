## Overview

The **BMP-2** is a second-generation Soviet amphibious IFV. Introduced in Alpha 15.0, it improves upon the BMP-1 with a 30mm autocannon and modern Konkurs ATGM. Comparable to Western IFVs like M2A3 and FV510.

**Key Features:**

- **30mm 2A42 autocannon** - High rate of fire, 500 total rounds
- **9M113 Konkurs ATGM** - 4 missiles (except IMF)
- **Amphibious** - Crosses water obstacles
- **3 crew + 7 passengers** - Full squad transport
- **GFI uses weaker AP rounds** (3UBR6 vs 3UBR8)

## Gameplay

- **Equivalent to FV510/M2A3** but lighter armor
- Don't face MBTs frontally (1250 HP = 2 APFSDS kills)
- M2A3 has 2000 HP and faster TOW reload
- 30mm effective vs IFVs and infantry
- Konkurs for armor - conserve missiles
- Front armor sloped but only stops .50 cal
- Hull sides may be safer vs MBT than front (engine hits)
- Be aware of ammo consumption at high ROF

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Support role** - flank rather than charge
2. **Use 30mm HE** vs infantry
3. **Use 30mm AP** vs IFVs and helicopters
4. **Reserve Konkurs** for enemy armor
5. **Face incoming fire** with front armor
6. **Transport infantry** - 7 passenger capacity
7. **High gun elevation (+74°)** - good vs helicopters
