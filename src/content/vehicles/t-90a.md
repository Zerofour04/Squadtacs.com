## Overview

The **T-90A** is a modern Russian main battle tank based on the T-72B platform. Introduced in V8.0, it serves as the top-tier MBT for Russian Ground Forces with improved armor and fire control over the T-72B3.

**Key Features:**

- **125mm 2A46M-5 smoothbore** - Same as T-72B3
- **9M119M Refleks ATGM** - Gun-launched missile
- **800mm penetration** - APFSDS
- **Autoloader** - 8s reload
- **Modern fire control** - Thermal sights
- **RGF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **Same as T-72B3** - Similar performance and firepower
- **8s autoloader** - Slower than M1A2's 6s
- **Very slow reverse** - 6 km/h limits retreat
- **ATGM capability** - 9M119M for long range
- **Carousel ammo** - Vulnerable to cookoff
- **Low profile** - Harder to spot and hit
- **Poor gun depression** - -6° limits hull-down

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Fire first** - Can't retreat quickly
2. **ATGM sniping** - Outrange enemy armor
3. **Use low profile** - Easier to conceal
4. **Protect flanks** - Carousel ammo vulnerable
5. **Know weak spots** - Gun mantlet + lower glacis
6. **Engine smoke** - Driver defensive screen
7. **Frag vs infantry** - 9 rounds available
8. **Commander HMG** - NSVT for light targets
