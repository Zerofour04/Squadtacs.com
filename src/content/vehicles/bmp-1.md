## Overview

The **BMP-1** is a Soviet amphibious tracked IFV. Introduced in Alpha 14.0, it's the first mass-produced IFV used by irregular factions. Armed with a 73mm low-pressure gun and Malyutka ATGM.

**Key Features:**

- **73mm gun** - HEAT + Frag rounds (similar to SPG-9)
- **Malyutka ATGM** - 4 missiles, slow but deadly
- **Amphibious** - Crosses water obstacles
- **3 crew + 7 passengers** - Full squad transport
- **No close-range defense** - Gun/ATGM have arming distance

## Gameplay

- **Weakest IFV** in duels vs autocannon IFVs
- ATGM fires from top - aim below center at short range
- **No close defense** - gun and ATGM have arming distance
- Keep HEAT loaded - fast switch to ATGM when needed
- No turret smoke - only smoke generator
- Front armor heavily sloped - best protection
- Always face incoming fire
- **GFI BMP-1 has vertical stabilization** (not horizontal)

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Avoid open duels** vs modern IFVs
2. **Ambush tactics** - first shot advantage
3. **Use ATGM** vs vehicles, 73mm vs infantry
4. **Stationary firing** - no stabilization
5. **Driver-Gunner communication** essential
6. **Transport infantry** - 7 passenger capacity
7. **Rear/side hits** with ATGM can kill MBTs
