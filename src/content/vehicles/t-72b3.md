# T-72B3

The **T-72B3** is a modernized main battle tank used by Russian Ground Forces and VDV in Squad. It features an upgraded fire control system, thermal sights, and Kontakt-5 explosive reactive armor, making it a formidable presence on the battlefield.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Gun-launched 9M119M ATGM for long-range engagements
- High ammunition variety (APFSDS, HEAT, Frag, ATGM)
- Kontakt-5 ERA provides excellent frontal protection
- Commander's NSVT 12.7mm effective against infantry and light vehicles
- Good top speed on roads (79 km/h)

**Weaknesses:**
- Very slow reverse speed (6 km/h) limits tactical flexibility
- Autoloader carousel vulnerable to ammo cook-off when penetrated
- Lower profile limits gun depression in hull-down positions
- Side and rear armor relatively thin

---

## Tactical Notes

- The 9M119M ATGM can engage targets at extreme ranges - use it to outrange enemy tanks
- Avoid exposing your flanks; the slow reverse makes retreating difficult
- Use Frag rounds against infantry concentrations and light fortifications
- Commander's NSVT is not stabilized - stop the tank for accurate fire
- ERA provides one-time protection - subsequent hits to the same area will penetrate
- Available to both RGF and VDV factions
