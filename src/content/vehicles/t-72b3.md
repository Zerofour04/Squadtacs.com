## Overview

The **T-72** is a Soviet main battle tank that entered service in 1973. One of the most widely-produced tanks in history with over 25,000 built. In Squad, it is represented by two variants: the T-72B3 for RGF/VDV and the T-72S for GFI.

**Key Features:**

- **125mm 2A46 smoothbore** - Same caliber as T-90A
- **Autoloader** - 8s reload, no loader crew
- **Carousel ammo storage** - Vulnerable to cookoff
- **800mm penetration** - APFSDS
- **ATGM capability** - T-72B3 only (9M119M Refleks)
- **Very slow reverse** - Only 6 km/h

## Gameplay

- **8s autoloader** - Slower than M1A2's 6s human loader
- **Very slow reverse** - 6 km/h makes retreating dangerous
- **Carousel ammo** - Horizontal storage, vulnerable at lower hull
- **No load indicator voice** - Audio cues only
- **ATGM for sniping** - T-72B3 Refleks useful at range
- **Low profile** - Harder to hit, easier to conceal
- **Poor gun depression** - -6° limits hull-down positions

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Fire first** - Can't retreat quickly
2. **Avoid frontal duels** - 8s vs 6s reload disadvantage
3. **Use low profile** - Conceal easier than M1A2
4. **ATGM sniping** - Outrange with Refleks (T-72B3)
5. **Protect flanks** - Carousel ammo vulnerable
6. **Know weak spots** - Gun mantlet + lower glacis
7. **Engine smoke** - Driver-activated defensive screen
8. **Frag rounds** - T-72S has 22 for infantry
