# CTM131

The **CTM131** (FAW MV3) is a third-generation tactical utility truck built by Shanghai First Automobile Works for the PLA. It was the first transport truck in Squad to feature machine guns for self-defense.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- First truck with self-defense machine guns
- Double cab seats 5 passengers
- High supply capacity (Logistics variant)
- Available to all PLA factions

**Weaknesses:**
- Weak armor like all trucks
- Exposed gunner positions
- Vulnerable to AT weapons and MG fire

---

## Tactical Notes

- The CTM131 was introduced in v4.0 as part of the Red Star Rising update
- The QJZ89 variant can engage helicopters, recon vehicles, and enemy transport
- Park behind walls to protect the truck bed while the gunner provides overwatch
- The double cab offers some protection but is still vulnerable to concentrated fire
- Available to PLA, PLANMC, and PLAAGF factions with different camo patterns
