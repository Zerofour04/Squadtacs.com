## Overview

The **CTM131** (FAW MV3) is a third-generation tactical truck developed by Shanghai First Automobile Works for the PLA. Introduced in V4.0 (Red Star Rising), it was the first transport truck in Squad with self-defense machine guns.

**Key Features:**

- **3 variants** - Logistics, QJY88, QJZ89
- **First armed truck** - Self-defense MGs
- **3000 supply capacity** - Flexible loading (ammo OR build)
- **Double cabin** - 5 passengers inside
- **All PLA factions** - Different camo patterns
- **12.7mm HMG option** - QJZ89 variant

## Gameplay

- **First armed logi truck** in Squad
- **Flexible loading** - 3000 ammo OR build points
- **Double cabin** - Better than single-cab trucks
- **QJZ89 variant** - Effective vs light targets
- Weak armor like all trucks
- Gunner exposed to all fire
- MG fire / AT weapons destroy quickly
- Different camo per faction

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **QJZ89** - Anti-air capable vs helicopters
2. **Park smart** - Wall protects truck, gunner overwatches
3. **Double cabin** - 5 protected seats
4. **3000 capacity** - Efficient logi runs
5. **Gunner covers** dismount operations
6. **Avoid combat** - Light armor only
7. **Engine weak** - MG belt to grill disables
