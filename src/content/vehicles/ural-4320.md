## Overview

The **Ural-4320** is a Soviet 6x6 off-road truck added in Alpha A9. It replaced the Ural-375D for Russian factions and is now used by GFI (Ground Forces of Iran).

**Key Features:**

- **Two variants** - Transport and Logistics
- **85 km/h** - Faster than Ural-375D
- **No armor** - Vulnerable to all weapons
- **No weapons** - Must be protected
- **GFI only** - Replaced Ural-375D for modern factions

## Gameplay

- **No weapons** - Must be protected by other vehicles
- **Weak armor** - Anti-armor weapons do serious damage
- **Engine vulnerable** - MG belt to front grill disables
- **Faster than 375D** - Better acceleration and top speed
- **GFI only** - Modern faction truck

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **No protection** - Avoid combat zones
2. **Fast logi runs** - Drop supplies quickly
3. **Engine weak point** - Front grill vulnerable to MGs
4. **Escort needed** - Cannot defend itself
5. **Better speed** - 85 km/h vs 375D's 75 km/h
6. **Quick transport** - Move squads rapidly

---

## History

The Ural-4320 was designed by Ural Automotive Plant in 1976 to succeed the Ural-375D. It was adopted by the Soviet Army in 1977 and served until 1991. RGF continued using it for transport, logistics, and as a BM-21 Grad platform. In Squad, it replaced the Ural-375D for modern factions.
