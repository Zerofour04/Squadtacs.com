# LPPV (Light Protected Patrol Vehicle)

The LPPV is based on the Ocelot/Foxhound vehicle, providing protected mobility for British forces. It features a V-shaped hull for mine protection.

## Specifications

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Tickets | 5 |
| Crew | 2 (Driver, Gunner) |
| Passengers | 4 |
| HP | 750 |
| Respawn | 5 min |

## Variants

### LPPV (Standard)
- Open top configuration
- No mounted weapon

### LPPV RWS
- **Armament**: M2A1 .50 cal in Remote Weapon Station
- Protected gunner position

## Features

- V-shaped hull for IED protection
- Good road speed
- Compact and maneuverable
- Limited off-road capability

## Tactical Notes

The LPPV is best used for rapid transport and light patrol duties. The RWS variant can provide fire support but should avoid heavy combat.
