## Overview

The **LPPV** (Light Protected Patrol Vehicle), also known as **Ocelot** or **Foxhound**, is the British light attack vehicle. Introduced in V2 for British Armed Forces, it provides protected mobility with IED-resistant V-shaped hull.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - RWS (.50 cal) and L7A2 (dual GPMG)
- **No crewman kit** required
- **6 passengers** - Squad transport
- **V-hull design** - Mine/IED protection
- **Fast transport** - High speed, quick acceleration
- **BAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **No crewman kit** - Any kit can drive/gun
- **RWS variant** - Gunner protected, .50 cal
- **L7A2 variant** - 2 exposed gunners, GPMGs
- **Light armor** - Small arms/grenade protection
- **Fast transport** - Quick in/out of combat
- **Vulnerable** - HMGs, AT, APCs, IFVs destroy it fast

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **RWS** - Engage light vehicles, protected gunner
2. **L7A2** - Anti-infantry, gunners exposed
3. **Fast transport** - Deliver squads quickly
4. **Light armor** - Avoid heavy weapons
5. **No crewman** - Anyone can operate
6. **Similar to M-ATV** - Same role, different faction
7. **V-hull** - IED protection design
8. **Don't brawl** - Only 750 HP
