# M113A3 TLAV

The **M113A3 TLAV** (Tracked Light Armored Vehicle) is a tracked armored personnel carrier used by the Canadian Armed Forces in Squad. It features a remote weapon station with both .50 cal and 7.62mm weapons.

## Variants

### M113A3 TLAV (RWS)
- Remote weapon station with M2A1 + C6 coax
- Requires Crewman kit
- Best protected variant

### M113A3 M2 (Open Top)
- Open-top M2A1 with frontal shield
- Low side protection for gunner
- Does not require Crewman kit

### M113A3 C6 (Open Top)
- Open-top C6 with frontal shield
- Low side protection for gunner
- Does not require Crewman kit

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Low ticket cost (5 tickets)
- Tracked for better off-road mobility
- Fast respawn time
- RWS version protects gunner

**Weaknesses:**
- Light armor (vulnerable to heavy MGs)
- Slower than wheeled APCs
- Open-top variants expose gunner
- Limited ammo capacity

---

## Tactical Notes

- The M113 is similar to the Russian MT-LB family
- Tracks provide better mobility in rough terrain than wheels
- Good for smaller map layers where LAV III is unavailable
- Use for infantry transport and light fire support
- All variants have 600 ammo points and smoke for driver
