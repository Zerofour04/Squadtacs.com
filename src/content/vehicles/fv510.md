## Overview

The **FV510 Warrior** is the British infantry fighting vehicle. Added in V11 for British Armed Forces, it features the 30mm RARDEN cannon shared with the FV107 Scimitar.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - Standard and Up-Armored (UA)
- **30mm RARDEN** - 6-round clips, manual reload
- **3 crew** - Driver, gunner, commander
- **7 passengers** - Infantry transport
- **No stabilization** - No hunter-killer
- **BAF exclusive**

## FV510 UA (Up-Armored)

The **UA variant** has additional applique armor on front and sides.

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| **STANAG Level 6** | No 30mm AP penetrates |
| **Front plates** | Immunity to autocannons |
| **Side plates** | Enhanced protection |

**Note:** UA variant can be more aggressive - armor upgrade is significant.

## RARDEN Mechanics

- **6-round clips** - Must press R after each clip
- **2 ammo types** - 120 APDS + 120 HE-T
- **Slow ROF** - Deliberate shots required
- **+1 capacity** - When reloaded before empty
- **Press 4** - Switch to smoke launcher
- **Press 1** - Switch back to main gun

## Tactical Tips

1. **Clip management** - Reload after 6 rounds
2. **Protect flanks** - Weak side/rear armor
3. **UA = aggressive** - Armor upgrade matters
4. **Turret weakness** - Enemy will target it
5. **No stabilization** - Stop to fire
6. **Smoke escape** - 2 charges, don't panic fire both
7. **Use rangefinder** - Effective at distance
