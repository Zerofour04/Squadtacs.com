# FV510 Warrior

The FV510 Warrior is the British Army's primary infantry fighting vehicle, providing armored transport with significant firepower from its 30mm RARDEN cannon.

## Specifications

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Tickets | 8 |
| Crew | 3 (Driver, Gunner, Commander) |
| Passengers | 7 |
| HP | 1500 |
| Respawn | 10 min |

## Armament

- **Main Gun**: 30mm RARDEN Cannon (AP + HE)
- **Coaxial**: L94A1 7.62mm

## Features

- Heavy armor for an IFV
- RARDEN cannon effective against light armor
- Slower rate of fire than autocannons
- Good infantry transport capacity

## Tactical Notes

The Warrior provides excellent protection for infantry dismounts. The RARDEN cannon's slower rate of fire requires careful shot placement.
