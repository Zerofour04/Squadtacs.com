## Overview

The **MT-LB** is a Soviet multi-purpose amphibious tracked APC. Adopted in the late 1960s, it replaced the AT-P. The MT-LBM is the modernized variant still in Russian service. Added in A9.

**Key Features:**

- **4 variants** - PKT, Logistics, ZU-23-2, NSVT (VMK)
- **Amphibious** - Fully water-capable
- **10 passengers** - APC variants
- **Light armor** - 14mm, stops small arms
- **Tracked** - Good off-road
- **Multi-faction** - RGF, INS, IMF, GFI

## Gameplay

- **Battle taxi** - Drop troops, retreat
- **Light armor** - Survives small arms
- **.50 cal vulnerable** - Don't fight HMGs
- **Slow and loud** - Poor stealth
- **ZU-23-2** - Mobile anti-air platform
- **VMK** - Heavy MG vs infantry/technicals
- **Logistics** - Armored supply run

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Drop and retreat** - Don't stay in combat
2. **ZU-23-2 standoff** - Engage at range
3. **VMK vs infantry** - NSVT effective
4. **Logi with armor** - Safer supply runs
5. **Avoid IFVs** - 30mm destroys fast
6. **Exposed gunners** - ZU-23-2 crew visible
7. **Use amphibious** - Cross rivers
8. **Slow turning** - Plan approaches
