## Overview

The **Z-9A** is a modernized utility helicopter serving as the primary aerial transport and logistics platform for PLA air units. Introduced in Squad v10.3, its sleek aerodynamic design allows for swift insertion of infantry squads and FOB radios into contested landing zones.

**Key Features:**

- **900 supply points** - Same as UH-1, less than UH-60M (1000)
- **No door guns** - One of three unarmed helis (with Z-8 and Loach)
- **Scout camera** - 360° co-pilot camera for long-range recon
- **Retractable landing gear** - Aerodynamic design
- **More nimble** - Better handling than other helicopters

## Tactical Tips

1. **Scout role** - Use 360° camera for recon
2. **No weapons** - Avoid hostile LZs, use escorts
3. **Nimble** - Use agility to evade threats
4. **Quick insertions** - Drop squads fast and leave
5. **Recon first** - Scout camera before landing
6. **Same supply as UH-1** - 900 total points
