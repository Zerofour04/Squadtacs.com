# TAPV

The **Textron TAPV** (Tactical Armoured Patrol Vehicle) is a light armored vehicle used by the Canadian Armed Forces in Squad. It serves as a reconnaissance and patrol vehicle with CROWS weapon station.

## Variants

### TAPV M2A1 CROWS
- **Weapon:** M2A1 .50 cal (400 rounds)
- Effective against infantry and light vehicles
- 2x zoom levels

### TAPV C6 CROWS
- **Weapon:** C6 7.62mm (750 rounds)
- Better anti-infantry with more ammo
- 2x zoom levels

---

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Protected CROWS keeps gunner safe
- Fast and maneuverable
- Good optics for long-range engagement
- Can withstand up to 12.7mm rounds

**Weaknesses:**
- Tall profile makes concealment difficult
- Only 4 passengers
- Cannot damage heavy armor
- Watch firing angle - can damage own vehicle

---

## Tactical Notes

- The TAPV is similar to the American M-ATV CROWS
- Best used for reconnaissance and light fire support
- The .50 cal variant can engage other light vehicles
- 2x zoom allows engagement at longer ranges
- Both variants have smoke launchers for defense
- Does not require Crewman kit
- Powered by 365hp Cummins diesel (110 km/h real life)
