## Overview

The **Textron TAPV** (Tactical Armoured Patrol Vehicle) is a non-amphibious armored car used by the Canadian Army. Based on the M1117 Armored Car, it serves as a reconnaissance and light fire support vehicle.

**Key Features:**

- **C16 40mm grenade launcher** - Main armament (Mk19 variant)
- **C6 7.62mm machine gun** - Secondary/coaxial
- **105 km/h** - Fastest light vehicle
- **12.7mm armor** - Stops heavy MG rounds
- **No crewman kit required** - Any kit can operate
- **CAF exclusive**

## Gameplay

- **Light attack vehicle** - Scouting and fire support
- **C16 grenade launcher** - Devastating vs infantry
- **Tall profile** - Difficult to hide in treeline
- **105 km/h top speed** - Very fast
- **No crewman needed** - Any kit can drive/gun
- **4 passengers** - Light transport capable

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use speed** - Fast flanking maneuvers
2. **C16 vs infantry** - 40mm grenades are deadly
3. **Avoid heavy armor** - Cannot damage tanks/IFVs
4. **Watch height** - Tall vehicle hard to conceal
5. **Smoke for escape** - 2 charges available
6. **Long range optics** - CROWS allows ranged engagement
7. **Light transport** - 4 passengers for squad mobility
8. **Recon role** - Spot and report enemy positions
