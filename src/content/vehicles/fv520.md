## Status: REMOVED

> **The FV520 was removed from Squad in V8.0.** This page documents the vehicle as it existed before removal.

---

## Overview

The **FV520 Warrior CTAS** was a semi-fictional experimental IFV based on the cancelled FV510 CSP (Capability Sustainment Programme). Added in V2.0 and removed in V8.0, it featured the revolutionary 40mm CTAS cannon with telescoped ammunition.

**Key Features (Historical):**

- **40mm CTAS** - Telescoped ammunition, high DPS
- **Turret stabilization** - Unlike FV510
- **Hunter-killer** - Commander target designation
- **FV510 UA chassis** - Same armor protection
- **FV4034 sights** - Same ballistics as tank gun
- **Was BAF exclusive**

## Why It Was Removed

The real-world FV510 CSP upgrade program was cancelled by the British Ministry of Defence in March 2021. The older Warriors would be replaced by the Boxer AFV instead. The FV520 in Squad was removed in V8.0.

---

## Historical Gameplay

- **Strongest IFV** - Near-guaranteed wins vs other IFVs
- **High DPS** - 40mm outperformed 30mm cannons
- **Low ammo** - Easy to run dry after firefights
- **Vulnerable to ATGMs** - IFVs with missiles could still win at range
