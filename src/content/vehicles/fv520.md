# FV520 CTAS40

The FV520 is an experimental Warrior variant equipped with the revolutionary 40mm CTAS (Cased Telescoped Armament System) cannon and Spike missiles. Often considered the strongest IFV in Squad.

## Specifications

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Tickets | 12 |
| Crew | 3 (Driver, Gunner, Commander) |
| Passengers | 6 |
| HP | 1750 |
| Respawn | 15 min |

## Armament

- **Main Gun**: 40mm CTAS Cannon (APFSDS + GPR-AB)
- **ATGM**: Spike LR (Fire-and-Forget)

## Features

- 40mm CTAS has exceptional penetration
- Spike missiles are fire-and-forget
- Full stabilization system
- Highest firepower of any IFV

## Tactical Notes

The FV520 can engage and defeat most enemy vehicles including some MBTs. The Spike missiles allow engagement from safe distances. This is a high-value asset that should be protected.
