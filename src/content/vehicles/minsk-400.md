## Overview

The **Minsk 400** is a Soviet-Belarusian motorbike based on the German DKW RT 125. Exclusively used by Insurgents, it's the most expendable vehicle in Squad and commonly used for IED attacks.

**Key Features:**

- **2 seats** - Driver + 1 passenger
- **92 km/h** - Third fastest vehicle
- **No SL approval** - Only vehicle anyone can take
- **1 ticket** - Very expendable
- **Fast respawn** - Quick replacement
- **IED platform** - Sapper vehicle bomb
- **INS exclusive**

## IED Attack Tactics

Sappers commonly use the Minsk 400 as a vehicle bomb:

1. **Strap IEDs** - Attach explosives to bike
2. **Drive toward enemy** - Maximum speed approach
3. **Detonate** - Trigger with detonator
4. **Expendable** - Sapper may or may not survive

---

## Rally Point Drops

Fast rally point deployment:

1. **SL + 1 squadmate** - Minimum for rally
2. **Ride to location** - 92 km/h speed
3. **Drop rally** - Squad spawns in
4. **Abandon bike** - It's expendable

## Gameplay

- **No protection** - Driver completely exposed
- **Any kit** - No crewman required
- **No SL needed** - Take freely
- **1 ticket** - Minimal loss
- **Very fast** - 92 km/h top speed
- **Off-road** - Works on any terrain
- **Loud** - Engine noise reveals position

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **IED runs** - Sapper special
2. **Rally drops** - SL fast positioning
3. **Flanking** - Speed behind enemy lines
4. **Scout ahead** - Expendable recon
5. **Abandon freely** - Only 1 ticket
6. **Use roads** - Faster on pavement
7. **Avoid combat** - Zero protection
8. **Park hidden** - Noise stops when off
