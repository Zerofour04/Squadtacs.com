## Overview

The **BTR-4** is a modern Ukrainian 8×8 wheeled infantry fighting vehicle. Introduced in V10.0 (Trident Strike update) for AFU, it combines mobility, protection, and firepower. First IFV in Squad with a fully modeled interior.

**Key Features:**

- **30mm autocannon** - Sustained suppressive fire
- **Coaxial machine gun** - Secondary armament
- **ATGMs** - Anti-tank guided missiles
- **8×8 wheeled** - High speed, road-capable
- **Full squad transport** - Infantry carrier capability
- **Modeled interior** - First IFV with full interior
