## Overview

The **UH-60M Grouse** (Black Hawk) is a utility helicopter in Squad, introduced in the A16 Update. Used by USA, ADF, and TLF (since V8.0).

**Key Features:**

- **9 passengers** - Plus pilot, copilot, 2 door gunners
- **1000 supply points** - 500 less than Mi-8/Mi-17/MRH-90/Z-8
- **M240H door guns** - USA/ADF (200-round boxes)
- **PKT door guns** - TLF variant (250-round boxes)
- **Protected pilot** - Cannot be hurt by regular gunfire

## Gameplay

- **1000 supply points** - 500 ammo + 500 construction
- **vs Mi-8/MRH-90** - 500 less supply capacity
- **9 passengers** - Large troop transport
- **Protected pilot** - Survives direct hits
- **Door gunners exposed** - Juicy target
- **Any kit** - Door gunners don't need crewman

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **9 passengers** - Full squad + more
2. **Protected pilot** - Aircraft survives longer
3. **Door gunner exposure** - Keep moving
4. **1000 supply** - Less than Mi-8 (1500)
5. **Banking passes** - Give gunners angles
6. **TLF PKT** - 50 more rounds per box
