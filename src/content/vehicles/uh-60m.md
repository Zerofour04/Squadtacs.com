## Overview

The UH-60M Black Hawk is a versatile utility helicopter used by the US Army and Australian Defence Force. It features powerful M134 miniguns that provide devastating fire support during insertions.

**Key Feature**: M134 miniguns offer significantly more firepower than standard door guns.

## Weapons Systems

### M134 Minigun (x2)
- 7.62mm rotary machine guns
- 1500 rounds per gun
- Extremely high rate of fire (~3000 RPM)
- **Devastating** against infantry in the open
- Limited traverse angles

## Tactical Tips

### For Pilots
1. **Fly low and fast** to avoid enemy fire
2. **Banking turns** give door gunners firing angles
3. **Plan insertion routes** to maximize minigun coverage
4. **Extract under fire** with suppressive passes first

### For Door Gunners
1. **Short bursts** to conserve ammo - miniguns burn through rounds fast
2. **Sweep the LZ** systematically
3. **Watch barrel heat** - sustained fire can cause issues
4. **Call out targets** to the other gunner
