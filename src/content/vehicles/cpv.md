## Overview

The **CPV (Convoy Protection Vehicle)** is an armored SUV used exclusively by WPMC. Introduced in V8.1, it's a semi-fictional vehicle loosely based on the GMC Yukon and Dillon Aero's CEV design.

**Key Features:**

- **2 variants** - Transport (unarmed), M134 Minigun
- **3 colors** - Black, Navy, Merlot
- **Up to 12 passengers** - 6 inside + 6 outside
- **Sideboard riders** - Exposed passengers outside
- **No crewman kit** required
- **WPMC exclusive**

## CPV M134 Minigun

Armed variant with open-top turret.

| Weapon | Caliber | Notes | Rounds |
|--------|---------|-------|--------|
| M134 Minigun | 7.62mm | Open-top, no sights | 800 |

**Capacity:** 10 passengers (4 inside + 6 outside)

**M134 Features:**

- **No sights** - RMB aligns view to barrel
- **Shift zooms** like other open-top weapons
- **Exposed gunner** - No protection from any direction
- **800 round belt** - Single continuous belt

## Gameplay

- **Fast transport** with light armor
- **12 passengers** (Transport) or **10** (M134)
- Outside riders **exposed** to all fire
- **Rollover = incap** for outside passengers
- M134 gunner has **no protection**
- Windows are **weak point** - damage hull
- Available in Black, Navy, Merlot colors
- Based on GMC Yukon/"DMG Duneli"

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Transport variant** - Maximum passengers, fast respawn
2. **M134** - Devastating ROF, but gunner exposed
3. **Avoid rollovers** - Outside passengers incapped
4. **Windows weak** - Can be shot to damage vehicle
5. **No sights** - RMB for M134 aiming
6. **3 min respawn** - Transport is expendable
7. **Protect gunner** - Keep moving
