## Overview

The **Z-8** (Changhe Z-18) is a tri-engine medium-lift transport helicopter manufactured by Changhe Aircraft Industries Corporation (CAIC). Introduced in Squad v4.0 (Red Star Rising), it is the largest helicopter in Squad with 27 total capacity.

**Key Features:**

- **27 total capacity** - Largest helicopter in Squad (as of V4.0)
- **1500 supply points** - Same as Mi-8/MRH-90, 500 more than UH-60M
- **No door guns** - One of three unarmed helis (with Z-9 and Loach)
- **Z-8J scout camera** - 360° co-pilot camera (PLANMC only)
- **Less nimble** - Practice on Shooting Range before combat

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Largest helicopter in Squad (27 total capacity)
- 1500 supply capacity (same as Mi-8/Mi-17/MRH-90)
- Z-8J variant has 360° recon camera for aerial reconnaissance
- Can transport entire squads in one trip

**Weaknesses:**
- One of three unarmed helicopters (with Z-9 and Loach)
- Less nimble than other helicopters
- Large profile makes it easy to spot and hit
- Vulnerable to all anti-air weapons

## Tactical Notes

- One of three unarmed helicopters (with Z-9 and Loach) - rely on escorts
- Z-8J variant (PLANMC exclusive) has a 360° scout camera for the co-pilot (like UH-1Y, CH-146)
- Use the massive 27-person capacity to transport multiple squads for coordinated assaults
- Less nimble than other helicopters - practice on Shooting Range before combat
- 1500 supply capacity (same as Mi-8/MRH-90, 500 more than UH-60M)
- Based on the French Aérospatiale SA 321 Super Frelon design via the Harbin Z-8
