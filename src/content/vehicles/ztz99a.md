# ZTZ99A

The **ZTZ99A** (Type 99A) is the main battle tank of the People's Liberation Army in Squad. It features a 125mm smoothbore cannon with gun-launched ATGM capability and represents China's most advanced domestically produced tank.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Gun-launched ATGM capability (4 missiles)
- High ammunition variety (AP, HEAT, HE, ATGM)
- 3-man crew with autoloader
- Stabilized RWS for commander
- Good frontal composite armor

**Weaknesses:**
- Autoloader carousel vulnerable to ammo cook-off
- Less crew redundancy (no loader)
- Lower profile limits gun depression
- Turret ring area can be vulnerable

---

## Tactical Notes

- The ZTZ99A can fire ATGMs through the main gun - useful for long-range engagements
- Autoloader means faster reload but ammo stored in hull carousel
- Commander's QJC88 12.7mm RWS is stabilized and effective against infantry and light vehicles
- Use the variety of ammunition types: APFSDS for armor, HEAT for stationary targets, HE for infantry
- Available to PLA and PLAAGF factions
