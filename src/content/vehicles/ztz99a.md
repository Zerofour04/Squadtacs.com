## Overview

The **ZTZ99A** (Type 99A) is the main battle tank of PLA and PLAAGF. Introduced in Squad v4.0 (Red Star Rising), it features a 125mm smoothbore with gun-launched ATGM capability. One of the strongest tanks in-game with front armor similar to M1 Abrams.

**Key Features:**

- **125mm ZPT98A smoothbore** - AP, HEAT, HE, plus 4 ATGMs
- **QJC88 12.7mm RWS** - Commander's stabilized HMG
- **3000 HP** - Same as other MBTs
- **ATGM capability** - Gun-launched missiles for long range
- **Strong frontal armor** - Front ammorack protected by >800mm
- **70 km/h** - Fast for a main battle tank

## Armor

- **Front ammorack**: Protected by >800mm armor
- **Similar protection to M1 Abrams** - one of strongest tanks
- **Weak spots**: Aim for <550mm areas for reliable penetration
- **T-62 note**: 115mm gun can only penetrate <550mm areas

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- Gun-launched ATGM (4 missiles)
- High ammunition variety (AP, HEAT, HE, ATGM)
- 3-man crew with autoloader (8s reload)
- Stabilized QJC88 RWS for commander
- Strong frontal composite armor (>800mm)

**Weaknesses:**
- Autoloader carousel vulnerable to ammo cook-off
- Less gun depression (-6°) than Western tanks
- Lower profile limits situational awareness
- Turret ring area can be vulnerable

---

## Tactical Notes

- ATGMs fired through main gun for 4000m+ engagements
- Autoloader = faster reload but ammo stored in hull carousel
- Commander QJC88 RWS effective vs infantry and light vehicles
- Use ammo variety: AP for armor, HEAT for stationary, HE for infantry
- Front ammorack well-protected like M1 Abrams
