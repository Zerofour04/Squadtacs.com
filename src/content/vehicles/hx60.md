## Overview

The HX60 is a military logistics truck based on the MAN HX series. It serves as the primary supply and transport vehicle for the Australian Defence Force and British Armed Forces.

---

## Variants

### HX60 Logistics
- **Capacity**: 3000 supplies (1500 construction + 1500 ammo)
- **Passengers**: 1 (driver only)
- **Role**: FOB construction, ammunition resupply

### HX60 Transport
- **Passengers**: 17 (driver + 16 troops)
- **Role**: Mass troop transport to objectives

---

## Logistics Operations

### Loading Supplies
1. Drive to main base or FOB with supplies
2. Press interaction key near vehicle
3. Select load construction/ammo

### Unloading Supplies
1. Drive to FOB radio
2. Press interaction key
3. Select unload construction/ammo
4. Supplies transfer to FOB

### Supply Points
- **Construction**: For building fortifications, HABs, emplacements
- **Ammunition**: For rearming AT, mortars, and infantry

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Protect your logis** - they are essential for FOB building
2. **Use transport trucks** for initial infantry waves
3. **Establish supply routes** between main and FOBs
4. **Don't overload FOBs** - spread supplies across multiple positions
5. **Coordinate with SLs** on supply priorities
6. **Avoid contact** - the HX60 has no weapons
