## Overview

The **T-64BM2 "Bulat"** is a modernized Ukrainian main battle tank, upgraded from the Soviet T-64 platform. Introduced in V10.0 (Trident Strike update), it serves as a heavyweight spearhead for armored assaults for the Armed Forces of Ukraine.

**Key Features:**

- **125mm smoothbore gun** - Same caliber as modern Russian MBTs
- **Reactive armor** - Improved protection over base T-64
- **Modernized fire control** - Ukrainian upgrades
- **3-man crew** - Driver, gunner, commander
- **AFU exclusive**

## Background

The T-64 was the first Soviet main battle tank with composite armor and an autoloader. The BM2 "Bulat" is a Ukrainian modernization featuring:

- Kontakt-5 explosive reactive armor (ERA)
- Improved fire control system
- Enhanced thermal imaging
- Ukrainian-produced components
