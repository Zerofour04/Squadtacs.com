## Overview

The **T-62** is a Soviet main battle tank from 1961. It serves as the MBT for Insurgents, IMF, and GFI in Squad. First tank with a smoothbore gun firing APFSDS rounds.

**Key Features:**

- **115mm U-5TS smoothbore** - First smoothbore tank gun
- **Can carry 4 passengers** - Only MBT with this feature (INS/IMF only)
- **550mm penetration** - Weaker than modern 800mm tanks
- **9s reload** - Slowest in game (~6.6 RPM)
- **Low profile** - Harder to hit
- **Engine smoke** - Driver-activated

## Gameplay

- **Older design** - Weaker than modern MBTs
- **Low profile** - Use hull-down positions
- **Frag surplus** - 20 HE rounds for infantry
- **Passenger carrying** - Unique MBT feature (INS/IMF)
- **Know armor weakspots** - 550mm can't pen frontal
- **9s reload** - Plan shots carefully
- **GFI NSVT** - Protected but unstabilized
- **INS/IMF DShK** - Exposed but has shield

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use Frag rounds** - 20 vs infantry
2. **Hull-down** - Low profile advantage
3. **Flank modern tanks** - Can't pen frontal
4. **Know weak spots** - 550mm limitation
5. **Engine smoke** - Driver can deploy
6. **Carry infantry** - 4 passengers (INS/IMF)
7. **Commander range** - Use analog marks
8. **Protect loader** - DShK exposed (INS/IMF)
