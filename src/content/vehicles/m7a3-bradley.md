## Overview

The M7A3 Bradley Fire Support Vehicle (BFIST) is a variant of the M2A3 Bradley optimized for reconnaissance and fire support missions. Trading passenger capacity for enhanced ammunition storage, it provides sustained firepower for extended engagements.

| **Commander** | Situational awareness | Independent periscope, Hunter-Killer capability |

## Weapons Systems

### M242 Bushmaster 25mm Autocannon

Enhanced ammunition capacity over M2A3:

- **AP (Armor Piercing)**: 70 rounds - For engaging armored vehicles
- **HE (High Explosive)**: 300 rounds - Extended suppression capability

### BGM-71 TOW Anti-Tank Guided Missile

- 2 missiles available (can resupply at repair stations)
- Wire-guided anti-tank missile
- Maximum range approximately 3,000m

### M240C Coaxial Machine Gun

- 7.62mm belt-fed machine gun
- 1000 rounds available (increased from M2A3)
- Effective against infantry

## Armor & Protection

Identical armor profile to M2A3:

- **Frontal Arc**: Good protection against heavy machine guns
- **Side Armor**: Vulnerable to RPGs and autocannon fire
- **Rear**: Minimal protection

### Smoke Systems

- Smoke grenade launchers for concealment

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Sustained fire support** - More ammo means longer engagements
2. **No infantry dependency** - Operate independently from transport duties
3. **Scout and engage** - Perfect for reconnaissance in force
4. **Use TOWs wisely** - Still only 2 missiles
5. **Support M2A3s** - Provide covering fire while they deploy troops
6. **Flank enemy positions** - 25mm devastates from unexpected angles
7. **Stay mobile** - Don't become a stationary target
