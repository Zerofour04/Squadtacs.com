# CH-178 Chinook

The **CH-178** is a heavy transport helicopter used by the Canadian Armed Forces in Squad on certain Afghanistan map layers. In-game it uses the Mi-17 model as a placeholder.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- High supply capacity (1500 points)
- Large passenger capacity
- More stable platform than CH-146
- Better for sustained logistics operations

**Weaknesses:**
- Less maneuverable than CH-146
- Larger target for enemy fire
- Only available on specific map layers

---

## Tactical Notes

- The CH-178 appears on Afghanistan-themed maps for CAF
- Uses Mi-17 model in-game (Mi-17V5 variant)
- Better for logistics runs due to higher supply capacity
- Door gunners have PKP MGs with 8x 100-round boxes
- Can transport up to 9 passengers plus 4 crew
- Mi-8/Mi-17 family can carry up to 24 soldiers or 4 tons cargo
- Top speed 250 km/h, service ceiling 4500m
