## Overview

The **BRDM-2** is a Soviet amphibious armored scout car. Introduced in A9, it's a fast light attack vehicle with 14.5mm KPVT HMG. Available in **3 variants**: Scout Car, UB-32 Rocket, and Spandrel ATGM.

**Key Features:**

- **Fast** - Second fastest vehicle in Squad
- **14.5mm KPVT** - Same firepower as BTR-80
- **Fully enclosed** - Crew immune to small arms
- **No Crewman kit** required
- **3 seats** - Driver, Gunner, Commander (5x periscope)
- **5 passengers** for INS/IMF variant

## Gameplay

- **Armor immune to small arms** - grenades too
- Occupants **cannot be shot** from outside
- Loses to APCs/IFVs with HMGs in direct fight
- Use **speed and ambush** tactics
- **Commander position** invaluable for spotting
- INS/IMF can carry 5 passengers (others 3)
- Very **maneuverable** - good for hit-and-run

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Scout role** - use speed to spot enemies
2. **Flank and ambush** - don't face armor head-on
3. **KPVT vs infantry** - devastating
4. **Spandrel for AT** - mobile ATGM platform
5. **UB-32 rockets** - aim for IFV sides/rear
6. **Fill commander seat** - dedicated spotter
7. **300 ammo points** - can resupply infantry
