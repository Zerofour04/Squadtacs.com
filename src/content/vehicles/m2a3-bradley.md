## Overview

The **M2A3 Bradley** is a US Army infantry fighting vehicle. Added in V11, it bridges APCs and MBTs - carrying 8 infantry while providing serious firepower including TOW missiles.

**Key Features:**

- **25mm M242 Bushmaster** - 300 rounds (70 AP + 230 HE)
- **BGM-71 TOW** - 2 wire-guided ATGMs (1750m range)
- **Hunter-Killer** - Commander marks, gunner engages
- **8 passengers** - Full squad + 2
- **2000 HP** - Strongest IFV armor
- **US Army exclusive**

## Hunter-Killer System

When both commander and gunner have stabilization ON:

1. Commander spots target
2. Commander marks position
3. Gunner turret instantly slews to target
4. Works both ways (gunner can mark for commander)

## Tactical Tips

1. **TOW discipline** - Only 2 missiles, save for armor
2. **Dismount early** - 8 passengers vulnerable inside
3. **Hunter-killer** - Commander scans, gunner engages
4. **Medium range** - 25mm effective, avoid close combat
5. **25mm for IFVs** - Save TOW for MBTs
6. **Reload TOW manually** - 2nd missile needs manual reload
7. **Avoid MBT frontals** - Flank or use TOW
8. **Smoke generator** - Hold LMB as driver
