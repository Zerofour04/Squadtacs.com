## Overview

The M2A3 Bradley Infantry Fighting Vehicle is a versatile tracked armored vehicle that combines infantry transport capabilities with significant firepower. Armed with a 25mm autocannon and TOW missiles, it can engage both infantry and armored targets effectively while transporting a squad of soldiers.

## Weapons Systems

### M242 Bushmaster 25mm Autocannon

The primary weapon fires two types of ammunition:
- **AP (Armor Piercing)**: 70 rounds - For engaging armored vehicles and light armor
- **HE (High Explosive)**: 230 rounds - Effective against infantry and light vehicles

### BGM-71 TOW Anti-Tank Guided Missile
- 2 missiles available (can resupply at repair stations)
- Wire-guided anti-tank missile
- Highly effective against tanks and heavy armor
- Maximum range approximately 3,000m

### M240C Coaxial Machine Gun
- 7.62mm belt-fed machine gun
- 800 rounds available
- Effective against infantry

---

## Armor & Protection

The Bradley features aluminum and steel composite armor:
- **Frontal Arc**: Good protection against heavy machine guns and autocannon fragments
- **Side Armor**: Vulnerable to RPGs and autocannon fire
- **Rear**: Minimal protection
- **Explosive Reactive Armor (ERA)**: Additional protection on some variants

### Smoke Systems
- Smoke grenade launchers
- Can deploy smoke screen for concealment

---

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use TOW missiles wisely** - You only have 2, save them for armored threats
2. **Stay at medium range** - 25mm is effective at range, close combat is risky
3. **Dismount infantry before combat** - Passengers are vulnerable inside
4. **Hunter-Killer capability** - Commander can designate targets for gunner
5. **Support infantry** - Your autocannon is devastating to enemy infantry
6. **Avoid MBTs** - Your TOW can damage them, but direct engagement is risky
7. **Flank enemy armor** - Side shots with 25mm AP can penetrate lighter vehicles
