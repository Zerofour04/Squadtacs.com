# ZTD05

The **ZTD05** is an amphibious assault gun from the Type 05 family, used by PLANMC and PLAAGF for rapid breakthrough amphibious operations. It was the first Mobile Gun System (MGS) added for Chinese factions in Squad.

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- 105mm cannon effective against all vehicle types
- Fastest water speed in Squad (30 km/h) - shared with ZBD05/ZSD05
- Planing hull for high-speed amphibious assault
- Large ammo loadout (40 rounds total)

**Weaknesses:**
- Light armor (1250 HP) - not a true tank
- No passengers - pure fire support role
- Vulnerable to MBTs in direct combat
- Long respawn time (15 min)

---

## Tactical Notes

- The ZTD05 is part of the Type 05 amphibious family alongside ZBD05 (IFV) and ZSD05 (APC)
- Use its 30 km/h water speed for rapid amphibious fire support during beach assaults
- The 105mm gun can penetrate most armor but lacks the punch of 120/125mm MBT guns
- Introduced in v5.0 as the first PLA Mobile Gun System
- Only available to PLANMC and PLAAGF - NOT available to mainland PLA
- Pairs well with ZBD05 IFVs for combined amphibious operations
