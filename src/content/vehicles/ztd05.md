## Overview

The **ZTD05** is an amphibious assault gun from the Type 05 family, used by PLANMC and PLAAGF. Introduced in Squad v5.0, it was one of the first Mobile Gun Systems (MGS) added to the game. Shares the hydroplane hull with ZBD05/ZSD05 for 30 km/h water speed.

**Key Features:**

- **105mm ZPL98A rifled gun** - Not smoothbore! 40 rounds total
- **30 km/h water speed** - Fastest in Squad (shared with ZBD05/ZSD05)
- **Mobile Gun System** - Fire support, not a main battle tank
- **No passengers** - Pure fire support platform
- **550mm penetration** - Lower than MBTs, know your target's armor

## Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- 105mm rifled cannon effective against all vehicle types
- 30 km/h water speed (fastest in Squad)
- Hydroplane design for rapid amphibious assault
- Large ammo loadout (40 rounds total)

**Weaknesses:**
- Light armor (1250 HP) - not a true tank
- No passengers - pure fire support role
- 550mm penetration - lower than MBTs
- Vulnerable to MBTs in direct combat
- Long respawn (15 min)

---

## Tactical Notes

- Part of Type 05 amphibious family (ZBD05 IFV, ZSD05 APC)
- Use 30 km/h water speed for rapid amphibious fire support
- 550mm penetration requires knowledge of target armor schemes
- Ammo types copied from T-62 as of v5.0
- Only for PLANMC and PLAAGF - NOT mainland PLA
- Pairs well with ZBD05 IFVs for combined amphibious operations
