## Overview

The **ACV-15** is a fully amphibious Turkish IFV/APC family. Introduced in V7.0 for the Turkish Land Forces, it comes in **3 variants**. Based on the M113 chassis but with twice the side armor (30mm vs 15mm).

**Key Features:**
- **Amphibious** - Can swim without preparation
- **Shortest IFV** in Squad at 5.26m - harder to flank
- **3 variants** - 25mm IFV, M2 APC, MG3 APC
- **10 total capacity** (2 crew + 8 passengers)

## Tactical Tips

1. **Use amphibious capability** for flanking routes
2. **Shortest IFV** - harder to hit from the side
3. **25mm AP** is effective vs light armor
4. **APC variants at 5 tickets** are cheaper transport options
5. **Double smoke** (driver + gunner) for 4 total charges
6. **Taller than FV107** - better view but easier to spot
