# Belaya

Soviet-era industrial complex featuring massive factory buildings, rail yards, and dense worker housing blocks. Urban combat dominates with tight corridors and vertical gameplay.

## Map Overview

Belaya is set in a Russian industrial zone during winter conditions. The map features a mix of large factory complexes, railroad infrastructure, and residential worker housing blocks. Infantry combat is the primary focus, with vehicles supporting urban pushes rather than dominating open terrain.

## Key Locations

- **Factory Complex**: The central industrial buildings offer multi-story fighting with many windows and rooftops
- **Rail Yard**: Open area with train cars providing cover, dangerous for infantry without smoke
- **Worker Housing**: Dense apartment blocks in the eastern sector, CQB-heavy
- **Lumber Mill**: Western compound with log piles and sawmill buildings
- **Storage Depot**: Southern warehouses with vehicle access

## Tactical Tips

- **Infantry-focused**: This map rewards coordinated infantry pushes over vehicle play
- **Vertical gameplay**: Control rooftops and upper floors for map dominance
- **Smoke is essential**: Rail yard crossings are death traps without smoke cover
- **FOB placement**: Hide FOBs inside factory buildings for protection
- **Flanking routes**: Use the housing blocks to bypass fortified factory positions
