# Mestia

Georgian mountain village nestled in the Caucasus mountains. Extreme elevation changes create challenging combat scenarios. Infantry-focused with limited vehicle access.

## Map Overview

Mestia is based on the real Svaneti region in Georgia. The map features a central village surrounded by steep mountain terrain. Verticality is the defining characteristic - controlling high ground is essential for victory.

## Key Locations

- **Village Center**: Dense cluster of stone buildings, the primary objective on most layers
- **Tower Houses**: Traditional Svan defensive towers offering excellent overwatch positions
- **Mountain Ridges**: High ground positions dominating the valley below
- **River Crossing**: Low ground vulnerable to fire from multiple directions
- **Terraced Fields**: Stepped agricultural terrain providing limited cover

## Tactical Tips

- **High ground wins**: Always prioritize elevation; shooting downhill is far easier
- **Marksmen shine**: Long sightlines make DMR and sniper kits extremely effective
- **Vehicle limitations**: Steep terrain restricts vehicles to main roads
- **Flanking is key**: Use mountain paths to bypass defended positions
- **FOB placement**: Build FOBs on reverse slopes, hidden from enemy overwatch
- **Smoke for crossings**: River and open fields require smoke to cross safely
