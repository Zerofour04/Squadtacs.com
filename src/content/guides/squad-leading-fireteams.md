At first glance, working with proper fireteams can feel intimidating. It might seem overwhelming, but once proper fireteams have been set up, you will only have to worry about **3 "chess pieces"** (Alpha, Bravo & Charlie) instead of 8 individual soldiers. Your gameplay experience will change dramatically.

---

## Introduction

**Move away from using fireteam leader assignment as a way to only get contact information from a few soldiers** or using it to elevate only your friends. There are more ways fireteam orders can be used.

Try putting a Rifleman & a Medic in with the Anti-Tank or Machine Gunner - those individuals can lift those other classes higher, make them stay alive longer, or make them deadlier.

Take your squad leadership to the next level by incorporating the following **4 keywords**:

| Keyword | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Composition** | Maximize soldiers' efficiency by pairing them to combine strengths and negate weaknesses |
| **Control** | Offload workload on subordinates to use their own initiative |
| **Command** | Communicate goals and intent for subordinates to accomplish tasks |
| **Coordination** | Deal with multiple things simultaneously without assigning by player names |

**Examples of Coordination:**
- Attack an objective while building a FOB
- Suppressing while flanking
- Defending in multiple directions simultaneously
- Flanking from multiple directions

### Recommendations

1. **Announce fireteams at round start** - Let inexperienced players know there's a command structure to follow
2. **Announce fireteam changes clearly** - Members often don't realize they've been reassigned
3. **Clarify expectations** - Explain how you expect to use fireteams to influence FTL & member behavior
4. **Don't wait for volunteers** - If you need a specific role and nobody speaks up, order someone to change. If they refuse without good reason, kick them

## CONTROL

A big concern players have is that they don't see the benefits - they only imagine the work. In reality, **when you have fireteams the workload is reduced** because you distribute/delegate responsibility.

This works with **inexperienced FTLs too** - you just need to give clear intent.

### Why Have FTLs?

| Reason | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Beacon** | A point on the map where fireteam members know they should operate near. Medics can distribute better when following an FTL |
| **Pointman** | A "do as I do" or "go after me" person. Inexperienced members have a model to follow |
| **Situational Command** | FTLs can make split-second decisions based on their situational awareness |
| **Micromanagement** | Experienced FTLs can optimize their team placement for efficiency |
| **Communication** | Fireteam members use local VOIP, reducing squad radio congestion |
| **Marking** | FTLs can place contact markers, alleviating the SL from having to mark everything |

### What You Must Provide to FTLs

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Trust** | Trust they will make the best decisions. Failure causes FTLs to become unmotivated |
| **Authority** | Avoid micromanaging their members. If unhappy with execution, tell the FTL - don't bypass them |
| **Empowerment** | Ensure members follow FTL orders. Kick disobedient members on FTL request |
| **Purpose** | Explain your intent and what role they play. Without purpose, they can't take initiative |
| **Service** | Support their needs - coordinate mortars, solve ammo problems, fulfill requests |

### Assigning a Fireteam Leader

**Factors that make someone UNSUITABLE as FTL:**
- **Tunnelvision** - Focusing on scopes or multiple tasks simultaneously
- **Stealth** - Roles that benefit from enemies being distracted
- **Target Acquisition** - FTLs are often pointmen and need quick-acquire weapons
- **Passiveness** - Roles that need to remain stationary

**Role Suitability for FTL (1 = Best, 5 = Worst):**

| Rank | Roles | Reason |
|------|-------|--------|
| **1** | R, GR, AT | Assault rifles with scopes. AT/GR can use FT orders for range estimation |
| **2** | ENG | Similar to rifleman but may lack scope. Benefits from stealth for FOB destruction |
| **3** | DM | Low rate of fire, not ideal as pointman. Better as flexible squad asset or scout team leader |
| **4** | AR, MG | Must remain stationary for accurate fire. Sensitive to fatigue |
| **5** | MED | Casualties don't move - medics end up in the back treating wounded |

> **Note:** Experienced FTLs can overcome role limitations. Even inexperienced FTLs can serve as Beacon, Pointman, enable Communication Compartmentalization, and Mark targets - 4 out of 5 benefits!

## COMMAND

Your two greatest tools:
- **Your spawn menu map** - Place visual orders and markers
- **Your voice** - Direct, explain, instruct, and motivate

**A combination of visual and audible aids provides the best chances of getting your message through!**

### Commander's Intent

This method is known as **"Mission Command"** - leaders declare their goals (with restrictions) but not the specific actions to accomplish them.

<img src="/img/guides/fox-sqleading/commander-intent.png" alt="Commander's Intent" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; object-fit: contain; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1rem 0;" />

### How to Convey Intent

**1. Verbalize your intent:**
- **Brief** - Tell your squad the plan at round start. Explain what each team should prepare for
- **Re-Verbalize** - When situations change, explain how it affects fireteams
- **Prepare Orders** - "Team Bravo, be ready to counter-attack when you have contact"

**2. Show your intent (Fireteam Orders):**

| Order | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Arrow/Move** | Go here, prepare to move further |
| **Shield/Defend** | Move to location and stay/wait |
| **Dagger/Attack** | Clear from enemies, Search and Destroy |
| **Shovel/Dig** | Take down/Build HAB/FOB |
| **Eye/Observe** | Watch this direction, point of interest |

**3. Illustrate your intent (Map Markers):**
- **Waypoint Arrow** - Paint front lines, avenues of approach
- **Point of Interest** - Reference points for discussions
- **Pickup Marker** - Squad meetup for transportation
- **FOB Marker** - Prepare for supply drop and construction

**4. Gesture your intent:**
- If you start doing something, you become the example
- Soldiers will follow your lead if you haven't been clear

> **Important:** Have tolerance for misinterpretations. Blame yourself when subordinates don't understand - ask what YOU could have done differently.

### Verbal Communication

Communications should be: **Short, Brief & Clear**

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Short** | Keep communications short to leave room for higher priority events |
| **Brief** | Get maximum information without sacrificing brevity |
| **Clear** | Misunderstandings cost time and tickets. Articulate properly, address recipients |

**Communication Techniques:**
- **Repeat** - Say important messages 3 times to get through loud environments
- **Correction** - When you make mistakes, say "CORRECTION" followed by the correct message 2-3 times
- **Addressing** - Use proper callsigns: "Alpha", "Bravo", "Charlie" or FTL names. Avoid "can anyone" or "someone"

## COORDINATION

The key to success: **Don't become 2-3 small squads** - be one squad with elements helping each other.

> Give one fireteam the same mission as the squad and let other fireteams support/enable the primary fireteam.

**Distance Guidelines:**
- **100m** - Dense vegetation or urban terrain
- **200m** - Open terrain with little cover
- **300m** - Complete open terrain

### Movement

When walking on foot, mind your spacing. Moving in formation provides flexibility when engaged.

### Formations

**Formation Rules:**
- The Squad Leader doesn't need to be part of the formation
- The "Pointman" is always in the right position

| Formation | Speed | Security | Best For |
|-----------|-------|----------|----------|
| **Column/File** | FAST | MEDIUM | Fast movement, countering ambushes, flanking flexibility |
| **Staggered Column** | FAST | MEDIUM | Low risk movement, transforms into Line/Wedge |
| **Line** | SLOW | LOW | Assaults, ambushes, maximum frontal firepower |
| **Wedge** | MEDIUM | HIGH | Assaults when enemy expected to front, transforms into Line |

**Formation Commands (Rookie-Friendly):**

**Column:** *"BRAVO IN FRONT, LEAD THE WAY. CHARLIE FOLLOW BRAVO AT DISTANCE OF FIFTY METERS"*

**Staggered Column:** *"BRAVO LEFT OF SL, CHARLIE RIGHT OF BRAVO AT DISTANCE OF FIFTY METERS"*

**Line:** *"SQUAD ON LINE, BRAVO LEFT, CHARLIE RIGHT OF BRAVO"*

**Wedge:** Same as Line - a wedge is basically a "lazy line" when moving with inexperienced members.

## Attacking & Defending

What determines victory is your ability to **kill all enemy spawn points before they kill yours**.

**Key Principles:**
- Determine where enemy spawn points are
- Hide your own spawn points
- Have more spawn points than the enemy
- Divert some forces to spawn point "Search and Destroy" while others pressure the cap-zone
- Add distractions and diversions
- Coordinate with other squads - split responsibilities, treat other SLs with respect

1. **Organize your squad** with proper fireteam composition
   - Assault Team: AT, GR, R & MED (AT as FTL)
   - Support Team: R, MG, MED (R as FTL)
   - Scout Team: DM/ENG
2. **Keep your squad together** working on the same objective
3. **Coordinate** so 1-2 fireteams complete the objective while 1-2 support
4. **Don't leave fireteams without orders**
5. **Care about your FTLs**

### "The Speech"

At round start, establish expectations:

> **"Everyone welcome to my squad!**
>
> You have joined a NO BS squad. If you are doing stupid stuff, I will kick you without warnings.
>
> New players are welcome - but not using your eyes, ears, mouth and brain is not acceptable.
>
> **I have appointed fireteam leaders.**
>
> *If you are a fireteam leader:* I don't expect you to lead, but I encourage it. I will kick disobedient members on your request.
>
> *If you are a fireteam member:* You must stay in local range of your fireteam leader. Do not separate unless ordered."

**Purpose of the speech:**
- Shows you're serious and won't tolerate disruption
- No warning system prevents players from testing limits
- Empowers FTLs with kick authority
- Sets clear expectations for fireteam cohesion
