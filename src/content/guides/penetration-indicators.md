Understanding whether your shots penetrate enemy armor is crucial for effective vehicle combat. This guide shows you how to recognize penetration vs non-penetration hits.

---

## Impact Effects

When you hit an armored vehicle, the visual effect tells you whether your round penetrated or bounced off.

### Penetration Hit

When your round **successfully penetrates**, you'll see:
- A **small, contained spark/flash** at the impact point
- The effect is relatively subtle
- This means your round is dealing damage to the vehicle

### Non-Penetration Hit

When your round **fails to penetrate**, you'll see:
- A **large, dramatic shower of sparks**
- The sparks spray outward from the impact point
- This means your round bounced off - **no damage dealt**

## Quick Reference

| Effect | Meaning | Action |
| ------ | ------- | ------ |
| Small spark | **Penetrated** | Keep shooting the same spot |
| Large spark shower | **Bounced** | Adjust your aim to a weaker spot |

---

## Tips

- **Watch your hits** - Don't just spam shots. Observe the impact effect to know if you're doing damage.
- **Adjust aim on bounce** - If you see the large spark shower, immediately aim for a different spot (lower plate, turret ring, side armor).
- **Angle matters** - Even weak armor can bounce rounds if hit at a steep angle.
- **Distance affects penetration** - Some rounds lose penetration power at longer ranges.
