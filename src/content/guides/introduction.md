# Introduction to Squad

Squad is a tactical first-person shooter that emphasizes teamwork, communication, and strategic gameplay. Unlike most FPS games, success in Squad depends heavily on coordination with your team.

## What Makes Squad Different

- **No Lone Wolves**: You need your team to succeed
- **Communication is Key**: Voice chat is essential, not optional
- **Slower Pace**: Tactical decisions matter more than reflexes
- **Teamwork Wins**: A coordinated squad beats skilled individuals

## Your First Game

1. Join a server with low population first
2. Join an existing squad (don't create one yet)
3. Tell your Squad Leader you're new
4. Stick close to your squad
5. Ask questions - the community is helpful!

## Basic Controls

| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|
| Local Voice | V |
| Squad Radio | B |
| Command Radio | G |
| Map | M |
| Spawn Menu | Enter |
