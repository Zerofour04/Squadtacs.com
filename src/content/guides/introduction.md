Squad is a tactical first-person shooter and spiritual successor to Project Reality (Battlefield 2 mod). Unlike most multiplayer shooters that throw "teamwork" around as marketing, Squad **requires** it.

## The Three Pillars of Teamwork

Games fall into three categories for teamwork:
- Not needed
- Helpful
- **Absolutely required**

Squad is firmly in the last category. If you don't understand, want, or can't execute teamwork, you'll have very little fun or success.

### 1. Communication

Squad solves communication simply and effectively. Every player has voice channels they can use. **Get a microphone** - it makes communication faster, easier, and more effective.

For detailed information on voice channels, callouts, and terminology, see the [Communication Guide](/guides/communication).

### 2. Squad Cohesion

Understanding communication means nothing if you don't use it with other players. Report enemy positions, share relevant information, and **see yourself as part of the squad**.

Without your squad, you're practically defenseless. In 99.99% of situations, you'll face superior numbers alone and die. With no Medic nearby, you'll cost the team tickets and have very little fun.

**Find a squad where at least the Squad Leader has and uses a microphone.** Ideally, everyone has one.

Beyond that, use common sense: don't all stand in one spot, cover different directions, and talk to each other.

### 3. Understanding Game Mechanics

You need at least a rough understanding of the rules - when you win and when you lose.

## Game Mode: AAS (Advance and Secure)

The primary game mode. Both teams capture and hold flag points in a specific order.

| Marker | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Orange Arrow** | Flag you can attack |
| **Purple Shield** | Flag you must defend |
| **No symbol** | Currently irrelevant flag |

Flags are neutralized and captured when you have **more players than the enemy** in the capture zone. If one team holds all flags, **Ticket Bleed** begins - the losing team constantly loses tickets until they recapture a flag or run out.

**Key difference from Battlefield:** In BF, every captured flag is a spawn point. In Squad, the only guaranteed spawn is **Main Base**. All other spawns are placed by Squad Leaders.

---

## Tickets

Tickets are the most important resource. A team with no tickets **loses the round**. Avoid wasting them.

| Event | Ticket Cost |
|-------|-------------|
| Player dies | -1 |
| Losing a control point (AAS/RAAS) | -10 |
| Capturing a control point | +70 |
| Capturing a neutral point (round start) | +20 |

### Vehicle Tickets

Destroyed vehicles cost tickets. This ranges from -1 for a motorcycle to -15 for an M2A3 Bradley. Fifteen tickets means you'd need to kill 15 players to compensate.

### Ticket Bleed

If one team holds all control points, **Mercy Bleed** kicks in - the losing team loses 50 tickets per minute to end the round quickly.

**Bottom line:**
- Don't throw your life away carelessly
- Use vehicles wisely
- Capture enemy points
- Give Medics a chance to revive you

## Spawning

There's only one permanent spawn: **Main Base**. All other spawns are placed by Squad Leaders.

### Rally Point

A portable spawn for your squad only. Key facts:
- Physical item that SLs place (costs 50 ammo to acquire)
- Can be picked up and repositioned
- Spawns in 60-second waves (30 second minimum wait)
- **Destroyed if an enemy is within 10m**

### Forward Operating Base (FOB)

A team-wide spawn with two components:
1. **Radio** - Enables building fortifications
2. **HAB** (Habitation) - The actual spawn bunker

| Situation | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| 2+ enemies within 10m | FOB slowly takes damage |
| Each enemy in range | +10 seconds spawn time |
| Radio at 80% health | FOB disabled until enemies leave |
| Radio critically damaged | 60-second recovery window |

If the recovery window expires, the radio is destroyed. You can also deliberately dig down your own radio to avoid the ticket loss from enemy destruction.

### Revive

**Incapacitated ≠ Dead.** When you're downed, you're not dead yet.

- **All players** can revive with bandages
- **Medics** revive 25% faster and have more bandages
- Revived players have very low health and need healing
- **No ticket lost if revived**

**When to give up:**
- No Medic can reasonably reach you
- Your position is overrun and you'd die immediately after revive
- Ask your SL where to spawn

> Please don't scream "MEEEEEDIIIIIC" ten times in a row.

## Ammo Persistence

You spawn with the same ammo you had when you died. To get full ammo, resupply at an ammo crate or ammo bag.

**Minimum spawn ammo:**
- 2 rifle magazines
- 2 bandages

Changing class in the menu also gives you only the minimum.

## Classes Overview

### Commander
One per team, elected by vote. Has special abilities:

**Conventional factions (CAF, UK, US, RUS):**
- UAV
- 155mm Artillery Strike
- A-10 Gun Run / SU-25 Rocket Strike

**Unconventional factions (Insurgents, Militia):**
- Heavy Mortar Barrage
- Controllable UAV Drone

Must be within 30m of a friendly HAB (conventional) or near a vehicle/HAB (unconventional). Death costs -5 tickets.

### Squad Leader (SL)
Leads the squad, coordinates with other SLs, places spawns, approves vehicle claims.

### Fireteam Leader (FTL)
Assigned by the SL to lead part of the squad. Can place fortifications. See [Squad Leading with Fireteams](/guides/squad-leading-fireteams) for details.

### Rifleman
Standard class. No specific role but versatile. Carries an **Ammo Bag** (100 points):
- Magazine: 2 points
- Bandage: 5 points
- Grenade: 10 points
- LAT rocket: 50 points
- HAT rocket: 100 points

### Automatic Rifleman
Machine gun operator with the highest sustained firepower. Bipod for stability.

### Medic
Revives 25% faster, has more bandages, and is the **only class that can heal**. Play defensively - dead Medics doom squads.

### Grenadier
Rifle with underbarrel grenade launcher. Can lob HE and smoke grenades over cover.

### Anti-Tank (LAT/HAT)
Takes out vehicles. Can also use HE rounds against entrenched infantry.

### Marksman
Squad-level sharpshooter with magnified optics. **Stay with your squad** - you're not a lone sniper.

### Combat Engineer
Builds/destroys fortifications faster. Carries mines, explosives, and a repair toolkit.

## Tips for New Players

### Don't Play SL Yet
If you're new, join existing squads first. Watch what good Squad Leaders do (and don't do).

### Play Medic When Available
Important role, good for beginners. But understand your role: if the Medics go down, the squad is doomed. Play defensively. Don't run through enemy fire to be a hero - two casualties is worse than one.

### Use Voice Comms
When you see an enemy, call it out. Use this format:

> **What + Where + Distance**

Example: *"Enemy squad, southwest of Flag X, 150m"*

Use cardinal directions from a reference point (flag, FOB, or SL position). Compass bearings only work when standing directly next to someone.

### Stay in Good Squads
First sign of a good squad: everyone has a mic. Second sign: the squad moves together. If your squad is scattered across the map, leave and find a better one.
