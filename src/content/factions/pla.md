# People's Liberation Army

<img src="/img/flags/PLA.webp" alt="PLA Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The People's Liberation Army represents China's ground forces with modern equipment including the formidable ZTZ-99 main battle tank - considered the best MBT in Squad. Features the new QBZ-191 rifle family.

## Strengths

- ZTZ-99 is the best MBT in game (S-Tier)
- ZBD-04A HJ-73C can fire dual TOWs
- Modern infantry weapons (QBZ-191)
- Strong combined arms capabilities

## Weaknesses

- QBZ-95 (older variant) has unusual ergonomics
- Learning curve for weapon systems
