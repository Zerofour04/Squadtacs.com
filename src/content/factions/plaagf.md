# PLA Army Ground Forces

<img src="/img/flags/PLAAGF.webp" alt="PLAAGF Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The PLAAGF represents support and secondary units of the Chinese army, equipped with the older QBZ-95 rifle family but still fielding capable armored vehicles.

## Strengths

- Still access to powerful vehicles
- QBZ-95 is reliable and accurate
- Good combined arms options

## Weaknesses

- Older infantry weapons than PLA
- QBZ-95 bullpup design takes getting used to

- **Sidearm:** QSZ-92
