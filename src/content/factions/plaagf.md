# PLA Amphibious Ground Force

The PLA Amphibious Ground Force (PLAAGF) is the land force of the People's Liberation Army. With approximately 975,000 active soldiers, it is the largest branch of the PLA and together with the amphibious units of the Navy forms the spearhead in landing operations.
