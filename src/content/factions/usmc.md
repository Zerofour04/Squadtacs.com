# United States Marine Corps

<img src="/img/flags/USMC.webp" alt="USMC Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The USMC is an expeditionary force specializing in amphibious operations. They feature unique vehicles like the AAV-P7 amphibious assault vehicle and LAV-25 for rapid deployment.

## Strengths

- Amphibious vehicle capabilities
- LAV-25 for rapid reconnaissance
- Good combined arms doctrine
- AAVP-7A1 for troop transport with firepower

## Weaknesses

- Less armored than Army counterparts
- Limited heavy armor options
