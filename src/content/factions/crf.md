# Canadian Resistance Forces

The **Canadian Resistance Forces (CRF)** are a fictional unconventional faction in Squad. This force of freedom fighters and guerrillas operates across the Canadian wilderness, using a mix of civilian and military equipment to wage asymmetric warfare. The CRF combines older military weapons with improvised vehicles and guerrilla tactics.

## Tactics

The Canadian Resistance Forces excel in guerrilla warfare and hit-and-run tactics:

- **Mobility**: Use light vehicles and knowledge of terrain to outmaneuver enemies
- **Ambushes**: Set up defensive positions along supply routes
- **Improvised Assets**: Make the most of mixed equipment and civilian vehicles
- **Asymmetric Warfare**: Avoid direct confrontation with heavily armored forces
