# Middle Eastern Alliance

<img src="/img/flags/MEA.webp" alt="MEA Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The Middle Eastern Alliance represents a coalition of Middle Eastern nations equipped with a mix of Russian and Western hardware. Features T-72 tanks and BMP-2 infantry fighting vehicles.

## Strengths

- T-72 is capable when played correctly
- BMP-2 provides good fire support
- Mix of Russian and Western AT options
- G3 rifle hits hard

## Weaknesses

- T-72 has exposed ammo rack
- Limited modern equipment
- MT-LB is weak transport

---

## Infantry Weapons

- **Primary:** G3A3, FAL, MG3, PKM
- **Launchers:** RPG-7, RPG-29, Kornet
- **Sidearm:** Browning Hi-Power
