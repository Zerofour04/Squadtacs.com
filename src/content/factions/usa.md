# United States Army

<img src="/img/flags/USA.webp" alt="USA Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The US Army is one of the most versatile factions in Squad, featuring a well-rounded arsenal of weapons and vehicles. Known for their advanced optics, powerful armor, and excellent fire support capabilities.

## Strengths

- Excellent optics across most kits
- Powerful vehicle lineup including M1A2 Abrams
- Good fire support options
- Bradley IFV with TOW missiles

## Weaknesses

- M4A1 has higher recoil than some counterparts
- Limited specialized equipment compared to other factions

---

| Vehicle | Type | Role |
|---------|------|------|
| M1A2 Abrams | MBT | Main Battle Tank |

| M1126 Stryker | APC | Transport |

## Infantry Weapons

- **Primary:** M4A1, M249 SAW, M240B
- **Launchers:** M72 LAW, AT4, Javelin, TOW
