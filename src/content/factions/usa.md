# United States Army

The US Army is one of the most versatile factions in Squad, featuring a well-rounded arsenal of weapons and vehicles.

## Strengths

- Excellent optics across most kits
- Powerful vehicle lineup including M1A2 Abrams
- Good fire support options

## Weaknesses

- M4A1 has higher recoil than some counterparts
- Limited specialized equipment compared to other factions

## Key Vehicles

| Vehicle | Type | Crew |
|---------|------|------|
| M1A2 Abrams | MBT | 3 |
| M2A3 Bradley | IFV | 3 |
| M1126 Stryker | APC | 2 |
| UH-60 Black Hawk | Heli | 2 |

## Infantry Weapons

- **Primary**: M4A1, M249, M240B
- **Launchers**: M72 LAW, AT4, Javelin
- **Sidearm**: M17
