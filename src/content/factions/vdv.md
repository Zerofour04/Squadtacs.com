# Russian Airborne Forces (VDV)

<img src="/img/flags/VDV.webp" alt="VDV Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The VDV (Vozdushno-desantnye voyska) are Russia's elite airborne troops. They feature unique light armored vehicles designed for air-drop operations, including the BMD family and the powerful Sprut-SD tank destroyer.

## Strengths

- Sprut-SD has MBT-level firepower
- BMD-4M is fast and well-armed
- Aggressive playstyle suits the faction
- Good anti-tank weapons

## Weaknesses

- All vehicles have light armor
- Sprut-SD can be one-shot easily
- BMD family is fragile
