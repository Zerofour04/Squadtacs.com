# Russian Airborne Forces

The **Vozdushno-Desantnye Voyska (VDV)** are the Russian Airborne Forces, an elite branch of the Russian military specializing in rapid deployment operations. The VDV operates unique air-droppable combat vehicles including the BMD family and the Sprut-SDM1 assault gun, making them one of the most mobile conventional forces in Squad.
