# Irregular Militia Forces

<img src="/img/flags/IMF.webp" alt="IMF Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The Irregular Militia Forces represent local armed groups fighting with older Soviet-era equipment and improvised weapons. They rely on numbers, knowledge of terrain, and asymmetric tactics.

## Strengths

- T-62 can still fight if used carefully
- BMP-1 can penetrate tanks
- Cheap ticket costs
- Knows the terrain

## Weaknesses

- T-62 is a meme tank
- Limited modern equipment
- Technicals are fragile
- No stabilization on vehicles
