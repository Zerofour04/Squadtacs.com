# Irregular Militia Forces

The **Irregular Militia Forces (IMF)** are a semi-fictional unconventional faction in Squad. This force of freedom fighters, paramilitary guerrillas, and deserters roams the Eastern European plains, causing chaos wherever they appear. Financed primarily through arms and drug trafficking, they possess a large arsenal of weapons and equipment making them a formidable fighting force. Unlike the Insurgents, the Militia uses somewhat more modern weapons where possible and has access to BM-21 Grad multiple rocket launchers.
