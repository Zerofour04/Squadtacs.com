## Strengths

- M1A1 Abrams is a solid MBT
- ASLAV for reconnaissance
- Accurate EF88 rifle
- Bushmaster PMV for protected transport

## Weaknesses

- Limited IFV options
- ASLAV has weak armor

---

## Key Vehicles

| Vehicle | Type | Role |
|---------|------|------|
| M1A1 Abrams | MBT | Main Battle Tank |
| ASLAV | IFV | Reconnaissance |
| Bushmaster PMV | APC | Protected Transport |
| MRH-90 Taipan | Helicopter | Transport |

## Infantry Weapons

- **Primary:** EF88 Austeyr, F89 Minimi, MAG-58
- **Launchers:** M72 LAW, Carl Gustaf, Javelin
- **Sidearm:** Browning Hi-Power
