## Strengths

- LAV 6.0 is a capable IFV
- Leopard 2A6M has excellent firepower
- Good infantry weapons with C7A2
- Coyote for reconnaissance

## Weaknesses

- Limited vehicle variety
- LAV 6.0 has low ammo capacity

---

## Key Vehicles

| Vehicle | Type | Role |
|---------|------|------|
| Leopard 2A6M | MBT | Main Battle Tank |
| LAV 6.0 | IFV | Infantry Support |
| Coyote | Recon | Reconnaissance |
| LAV III | APC | Transport |
| CH-146 Griffon | Helicopter | Transport |

## Infantry Weapons

- **Primary:** C7A2, C8A3, C9A2 LMG, C6 GPMG
- **Launchers:** M72 LAW, Carl Gustaf, TOW
- **Sidearm:** Browning Hi-Power
