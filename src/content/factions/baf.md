## Strengths

- Challenger 2 has excellent frontal armor
- Good infantry optics with ACOG and SUSAT
- FV510 Warrior provides solid infantry support
- Accurate L85A2/A3 rifle

## Weaknesses

- Limited vehicle variety
- Challenger 2 is slow and defensive
