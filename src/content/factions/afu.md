## Strengths

- Mix of Western and Soviet equipment
- NATO-standard AT weapons (NLAW, Javelin)
- T-64 is capable when used correctly
- Good combined arms options

## Weaknesses

- Learning curve for mixed equipment
- T-64 has exposed ammo rack
