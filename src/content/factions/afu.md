# Armed Forces of Ukraine

The **Armed Forces of Ukraine (AFU)** is the 17th playable faction and the 13th conventional army in Squad, added in Version 10.0 Trident Strike. Initially a large, neglected force with Soviet legacies, the AFU evolved into a combat-hardened military. Following Russia's 2014 annexation of Crimea, the AFU modernized and gradually adopted NATO standards.
