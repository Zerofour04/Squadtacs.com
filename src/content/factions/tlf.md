# Turkish Land Forces

The **Turkish Land Forces (TLF)** represent one of NATO's largest standing armies. As a major regional power, Turkey fields a unique combination of NATO-standard equipment and domestically developed vehicles and weapons. The TLF features the upgraded M60T Sabra main battle tank, domestically produced Cobra II and PARS III armored vehicles, and the modern MPT-76 battle rifle manufactured by MKEK.
