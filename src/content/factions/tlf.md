# Turkish Land Forces

<img src="/img/flags/TLF.webp" alt="TLF Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The Turkish Land Forces represent Turkey's modern military with a mix of NATO-standard and domestic equipment. Features the M60T Sabra main battle tank and Turkish-made vehicles.

## Strengths

- M60T is well-armed
- ACV-15 is a solid IFV
- Good mix of NATO weapons
- PARS III is fast

## Weaknesses

- M60T has exposed ammo rack
- PARS III is a meme (3x wheel damage)
- ACV-15 lacks TOW
