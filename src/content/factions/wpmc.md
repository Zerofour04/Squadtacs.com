# Western Private Military Contractors

The **Western Private Military Contractors (WPMC)** are a fictional playable faction in Squad. This is the 15th playable faction and the third generic unconventional faction in the game. WPMC focuses on non-standard, modern infantry. While they lack some of the assets available to conventional forces such as heavy vehicles and heavy weapons, they make up for this with speed, agility, and infantry firepower.
