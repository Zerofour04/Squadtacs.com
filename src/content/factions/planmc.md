# PLA Navy Marine Corps

<img src="/img/flags/PLANMC.webp" alt="PLANMC Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The PLANMC specializes in amphibious operations with unique vehicles like the ZTD-05 amphibious tank destroyer and ZBD-05 assault vehicle. Features the modern QBZ-191 rifle family.

## Strengths

- ZTD-05 can engage MBTs at range
- ZBD-05 HJ-73C has dual TOW capability
- Amphibious capabilities
- Modern infantry weapons

## Weaknesses

- ZTD-05 has limited penetration (550mm)
- Lighter armor than PLA ground forces
