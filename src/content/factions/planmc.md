# PLA Navy Marine Corps

The PLA Navy Marine Corps (PLANMC) are the ground forces operating under the command of the Chinese naval forces. Founded in 1953, the Corps has six Marine Brigades focusing primarily on power projection in the South China Sea and the Gulf of Aden. It is the **only faction in Squad without main battle tanks**.
