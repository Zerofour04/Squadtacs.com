# Russian Ground Forces

The **Russian Ground Forces (RGF)** are the land component of the Russian military. Formed from parts of the old Soviet Army after the collapse of the Soviet Union, today's Russian Army is a highly professional and technologically modern combat force capable of rapid overseas deployment. The RGF fields a diverse range of equipment from Soviet-era workhorses to modernized platforms.
