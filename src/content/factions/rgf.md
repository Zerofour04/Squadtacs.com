# Russian Ground Forces

<img src="/img/flags/RGF.webp" alt="RGF Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

The Russian Ground Forces represent Russia's conventional military might with a diverse array of armored vehicles, from T-72s to the modern T-90. Known for strong anti-tank capabilities and the iconic AK rifle family.

## Strengths

- T-90 is one of the best MBTs
- Excellent anti-tank options (Kornet, RPG-29)
- BMP-2 is a capable glass cannon
- Good infantry weapons with low recoil

## Weaknesses

- T-72 has exposed ammo rack
- Some vehicles lack modern optics
- BTR-82A has weak armor
