# Insurgents

<img src="/img/flags/INS.webp" alt="INS Flag" style="float: right; width: 120px; margin-left: 1rem;" />

Insurgents are unconventional fighters who rely on ambushes, IEDs, and knowledge of local terrain. They have limited conventional military equipment but can be deadly when using asymmetric tactics.

## Strengths

- IEDs can destroy any vehicle
- Cheap respawn tickets
- Can build hidden HABs
- Scout class for reconnaissance
- Unconventional tactics

## Weaknesses

- No tanks or heavy armor
- Limited AT options
- Technicals are fragile
- Relies heavily on ambushes
