# Insurgents

The **Middle Eastern Insurgents (INS)** are a semi-fictional unconventional faction in Squad. Originally a group of political dissidents, the Insurgents grew at an alarming rate following foreign intervention in their homeland. They now pose a significant threat, holding various strategic positions in rural areas and even penetrating urban zones. Masters of guerrilla warfare, they utilize a mix of Soviet-era equipment and captured Western weapons to combat their enemies.
